**Baron Ashbury** was an eastern nobleman from [Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)).

## History[]

### Lord of His Pack[]

He and [Genn](/wiki/Genn) grew up together, and he was once one of the king's most trusted friends. He was present in the [Capital City](/wiki/Capital_City) to create the [Alliance of Lordaeron](/wiki/Alliance_of_Lordaeron) and agreed with [Lord Godfrey](/wiki/Lord_Godfrey), to send only a token army. He participated in the hunts with Genn, [Lord Marley](/wiki/Lord_Marley) and Godfrey in the [Blackwald](/wiki/Blackwald).[1]

### Curse of the Worgen[]

**This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [manga](/wiki/Manga) or [comics](/wiki/Comics).**

One day before the attack on [Gilneas City](/wiki/Gilneas_City), he quarreled with [Lord Walden](/wiki/Lord_Walden) because of the bad situation in Gilneas especially the shortage of livestock.[2]

### Cataclysm[]

**This section concerns content related to *[Cataclysm](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm)*.**

He joined with Lord [Vincent Godfrey](/wiki/Vincent_Godfrey) to turn [Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane) over to the [Forsaken](/wiki/Forsaken) in exchange for an end to hostilities. Along with his ally, [Lord Walden](/wiki/Lord_Walden), Ashbury was slain by Gilnean [worgen](/wiki/Worgen) who fought to save their King. Godfrey, Ashbury, and Walden were all raised in undeath by the Forsaken and served them for a time. After Godfrey betrayed the Forsaken by slaying [Sylvanas Windrunner](/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner), Ashbury fled with his master into nearby [Shadowfang Keep](/wiki/Shadowfang_Keep).

## Quotes[]

On his undeath:

- I always loved Silverpine -- until the Lich King let loose his rampaging [Scourge](/wiki/Scourge) armies and turned the land into a rotting corpse pit. Now the sight of it boils my green blood.
- This is undoubtedly Godfrey's fault.
- They should have burned my damned corpse.
- Keep the conversations curt. I am in no mood for whining or rambling.
- Being forsaken has its benefits. Let me see... I am able to practically live underwater. I can stay submerged 233% longer than any fleshy mortal. Yes, count it, 233%.
- Oh, and now I am able to shrug off mind-controlling effects - BECAUSE I LACK A BRAIN!
- And perhaps the best part, I now have a hungering for the flesh of other humanoids. Cannibalizing their rotten corpses grants me power. Yes, DE-LI-CIOUS...
Random dialogue with Godfrey:

- Lord Godfrey says: For pity's sake Ashbury, cover yourself. Your leg bones are popping out.
- Baron Ashbury says: I did not bring this wretched affliction upon myself, Godfrey, it was a "gift," remember? Now kindly enjoy the gift and shut up.
