The **Greymane** family (also known as the **Greymane clan** ) [[ 1 ]]() is the ruling line of the [kingdom of Gilneas](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom) "Gilneas (kingdom)") . The family ruled from [Greymane Manor](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Greymane_Manor "Greymane Manor") and had a Royal Chemist in the form of [Krennan Aranas](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Krennan_Aranas "Krennan Aranas") .
