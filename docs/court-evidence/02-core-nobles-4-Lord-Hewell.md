**Lord Hewell** is a nobleman of [Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_peninsula) who can be found at [Tempest's Reach](/wiki/Tempest%27s_Reach) during the [worgen starting experience](/wiki/Worgen_starting_experience). Though the eastern lords ([Lord Walden](/wiki/Lord_Walden) and [Baron Ashbury](/wiki/Baron_Ashbury)) took part in [Lord Godfrey](/wiki/Lord_Godfrey)'s plan to take [King Genn Greymane](/wiki/King_Genn_Greymane) captive in order to bargain with the [Forsaken](/wiki/Forsaken), Hewell refused to betray his king. Instead he remained by Greymane's side until [Krennan Aranas](/wiki/Krennan_Aranas) sent an [adventurer](/wiki/Adventurer) to help.
