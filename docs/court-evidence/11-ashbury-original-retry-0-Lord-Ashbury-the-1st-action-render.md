**Lord Ashbury the 1st** was a [Gilnean](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)) noble and the father of [Baron Ashbury](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Baron_Ashbury). Lord Ashbury helped King [Archibald Greymane](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Archibald_Greymane) build Gilneas up to a nation of great power. King Archibald always told his son [Prince Genn](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Genn_Greymane) to trust in the Ashburys’ fidelity to the crown because of the accomplishments of Lord Ashbury.[1]
