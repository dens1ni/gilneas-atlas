# Lord Woofington

- **Lord Woofington**
- [Binds when picked up](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Bind_on_Pickup)
- Use: Teaches you how to summon this companion.

[File:Lord Woofington.jpg](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Special:Upload?wpDestFile=Lord_Woofington.jpg)

**Lord Woofington** is a [mastiff](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Mastiff) [companion](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Companion).

## Source[]

This item can be received as a reward from [Island Expeditions](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Island_Expedition) where feral [worgen](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Worgen) appear.

It can also be looted from ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/2/2e/Inv_misc_crate01.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/16?cb=20180818181053) [[Havenswood Salvage]](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Havenswood_Salvage).

## Pet Journal[]

*Lord Woofington was given his title after the rest of the denizens of [Havenswood](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Havenswood) succumbed to madness.*
