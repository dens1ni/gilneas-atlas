This article contains lore taken from Warcraft novels, novellas, or short stories.

Lord Ashbury the 1st was a Gilnean noble and the father of Baron Ashbury. Lord Ashbury helped King Archibald Greymane build Gilneas up to a nation of great power. King Archibald always told his son Prince Genn to trust in the Ashburys’ fidelity to the crown because of the accomplishments of Lord Ashbury.[1]
