**Brother Crowley** is a [human](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Human "Human") located in the [Cathedral of Light](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cathedral_of_Light "Cathedral of Light") in the [Alliance](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance "Alliance") capital city of [Stormwind](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Stormwind_City "Stormwind City") .
He is a member of the infamous [Scarlet Crusade](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Scarlet_Crusade "Scarlet Crusade") and serves as its ambassador to the [Kingdom of Stormwind](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Stormwind_(kingdom) "Stormwind (kingdom)") .
Before the events of the [Cataclysm](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cataclysm_(event) "Cataclysm (event)") , he was known to instruct [adventurers](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Adventurer "Adventurer") of the Alliance to seek out his colleague [Brother Anton](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Brother_Anton "Brother Anton") in [Desolace](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Desolace "Desolace") in the interests of the Scarlet Crusade.
He can be found in the lower levels of the cathedral, through a passage off the eastern side of the main hall.

* Brother Crowley may be named after the famous 20th-Century occultist [Aleister Crowley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleister_Crowley "wikipedia:Aleister Crowley") .
