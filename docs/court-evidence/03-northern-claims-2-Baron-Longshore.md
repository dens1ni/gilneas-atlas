**Baron Longshore** is the former captain of the *[Heedless](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Heedless)*, a ship in the [pirate](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Pirate) fleet of the [Southsea Freebooters](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Southsea_Freebooters), and hails from [Gilneas](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gilneas_peninsula).[1] He is located on the [Merchant Coast](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Merchant_Coast) in the [Northern Barrens](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Northern_Barrens).

## Background[]

[Gazlowe](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gazlowe) put a prize on Baron Longshore's head on charges of piracy and for leading vessels of the Southsea Freebooters.[1]

### Cataclysm[]

**This section concerns content related to *[Cataclysm](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm)*.**

After the Cataclysm, his fleet was decimated by the [Alliance](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance) navy. He and the surviving [Southsea Cutthroats](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Southsea_Cutthroat) can be found incarcerated by the [Northwatch Expeditionary Unit](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Northwatch_Expeditionary_Unit).[2] Horde adventurers are able to rescue him in a quest, in exchange for the location of his stolen treasure. He eventually double-crosses the adventurers and attacks them.
