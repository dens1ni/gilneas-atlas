**Calia Menethil**, styled "the Pallid Lady", is the daughter of [King Terenas Menethil II](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Terenas_Menethil_II) and [Queen Lianne](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lianne_Menethil), and the older sister of [Prince Arthas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Arthas_Menethil). Once the princess of the fallen [kingdom of Lordaeron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lordaeron_(kingdom)), she is now a member of the [Desolate Council](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Desolate_Council) of the [Forsaken](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken) after having been raised into undeath by the [Holy Light](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Light). She survived the [scourging of Lordaeron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Scourging_of_Lordaeron) and went into hiding for years, eventually reappearing as a [priest](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Priest) alongside the [undead](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Undead) [Alonsus Faol](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alonsus_Faol) during the [Burning Legion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Burning_Legion)'s [third invasion of Azeroth](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion), acting as a champion of the [Conclave](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Conclave).

Hoping to reunite with her lost husband and daughter, whom she had become separated from during the [Third War](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Third_War), Calia later worked with [King Anduin Wrynn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) to organize the [Gathering](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gathering), where undead Forsaken could get the opportunity to reunite with their living [human](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Human) relatives. During the event, Calia was slain by the Banshee Queen [Sylvanas Windrunner](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner), but was subsequently resurrected as an undead infused with the Light under the guidance of the [naaru](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Naaru) [Saa'ra](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Saa%27ra).

Alongside [Derek Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Derek_Proudmoore), to whom she had grown close in the event of the [Fourth War](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fourth_War), Calia eventually joined the Forsaken, assisted in the reclamation of the [Ruins of Lordaeron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ruins_of_Lordaeron), and now serves as a member of the reformed Desolate Council governing the faction.

## Biography

### Early life

[![WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [novels](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels), [novellas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novellas), or [short stories](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Short_stories).**

Calia is the eldest child of [King Terenas Menethil II](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Terenas_Menethil_II) and [Queen Lianne](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lianne_Menethil). She was two years older than her brother [Arthas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Arthas_Menethil),[5] who was born in year 4 [BDP](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/BDP),[6] which puts her birth at around 6 BDP. Although Calia was older, Arthas, as the firstborn son, was the heir apparent to the throne.[3] As such, Calia was never formally taught about politics, since no one ever expected her to rule.[4] Calia loved her brother[7] and the two got along "well enough".[5] She was doted on by her father,[8] but although Terenas was a kind and understanding man, he made clear that he would choose his son-in-law, and that Calia would agree to it.[3]

Calia was in the [royal palace](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lordaeron%27s_palace) with her family during the [Horde](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Old_Horde)'s [Siege of Capital City](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Siege_of_Capital_City) during the [Second War](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Second_War).[9] During the eleven-year-old [Jaina Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jaina_Proudmoore)'s brief stay in Capital City, she and the fourteen-year-old Calia became close friends.[10]

When Calia was sixteen, still two years away from marriage, Terenas planned to betroth her against her will to the [Alteraci](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alterac_(kingdom)) noble [Daval Prestor](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Deathwing), who was secretly the [black dragon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Black_dragon) [Deathwing](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Deathwing) in disguise. Deathwing hoped that by taking control of Alterac and wedding the daughter of one of the [Alliance](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alliance_of_Lordaeron)'s most powerful monarchs, he would put himself in a political position from which he could undermine and destroy humanity from within.[11][12] Calia was deeply upset at the idea of being forced to marry a man she didn't know, but Terenas, who was in secret being magically controlled by Deathwing, refused to accept her protests. Calia made Arthas promise not to do the same thing to his own children and to marry a woman who loved him.[8] Following the [Battle of Grim Batol](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Battle_of_Grim_Batol), Prestor disappeared, and the plans of his betrothal to Calia dwindled away.[13]

Five years later, Calia attended Arthas' ceremony to become a member of the [Knights of the Silver Hand](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Knights_of_the_Silver_Hand) in [Stormwind City](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stormwind_City). After the ceremony, she hugged her brother and told him that his family was proud of him.[14] As Arthas grew up and Terenas turned his attention toward him, Calia fell in love with someone her father would never have approved of: a Lordaeron [footman](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Footman). It was her way of bucking Terenas' plan for her; had she been heir to the throne, she would have gone along with it.

Once in the dark of night, they snuck away and begged a priestess to marry them; when the woman refused, the couple returned many times until she finally agreed. Eventually, Calia became pregnant and confided in her mother Lianne. While at first furious, Lianne could tell from Calia's face that it was true love and aided her in keeping her relationship and pregnancy a secret after she was assured that her grandchild would be legitimate. A few weeks after giving birth, it was decided that the father would raise their daughter away from Lordaeron, ignorant of her birthright. Lianne promised that once Arthas was properly wed and produced an heir, they would acknowledge Calia's daughter and possibly elevate her husband to a nobleman's status.[15][3]

Calia once listened to [Benedictus Voss](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Benedictus_Voss)'s sermons.[16] She had met and knew Archbishop [Alonsus Faol](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alonsus_Faol) well enough to easily recognize his face.[15] She and the great Lordaeronian historian [Parqual Fintallas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Parqual_Fintallas) also knew each other.[17]

### Third War and aftermath

[![WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [novels](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels), [novellas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novellas), or [short stories](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Short_stories).**

Calia was officially thought to have perished on the day Arthas murdered their father.[18] Arthas himself believed his sister to have died,[19] but in truth, she survived. Calia doesn't remember much of the time,[3] other than that she at one point hid in a muddy trench for two days to avoid prowling [ghouls](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ghoul).[20]

Eventually, Calia made her way to [Southshore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Southshore), where her husband and child had been hidden away and were safe from the Scourge, and tearfully reunited with her beloved family. With everyone assuming she was dead, she lived there in secret with her family for some time until the town was attacked by [blight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Blight). The three fled along with the rest of the town, but Calia was once again separated from her family in the crowd. Screaming their names in the middle of the street, a man pulled her onto his horse and galloped past the town limits barely in time, and she joined a cluster of refugees in the forest. She prayed she'd be one of those who would be reunited with their loved ones, but this time she never saw them again, not knowing their fate.

At some point, while escaping from mindless undead, Calia stumbled upon one who was still sane and whom she recognized. [Alonsus Faol](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alonsus_Faol), who was a [Forsaken](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken), showed her that not all undead had lost their minds, and there were some who still were themselves even after death. This made her understand that the Forsaken and the [Scourge](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Scourge) were two separate entities, with the former still deserving of dignity and compassion. That gave her the chance to move through her shock and pain to a place of healing. Seeing the Forsaken as her old friends still living, she hoped that if her family had been killed by the undead and raised, they would live on as Forsaken instead of mindless Scourge.[15][3]

### Legion

[![Legion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion) **This section concerns content related to *[Legion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion)*.**

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Calia_Menethil.jpg/240px-Calia_Menethil.jpg?1fa1d2)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Calia_Menethil.jpg)

Having become a [priest](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Priest) at some point, Calia reappeared during the [Burning Legion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Burning_Legion)'s [invasion of Azeroth](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion) alongside Alonsus and was initially encountered by priests at [Faol's Rest](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Faol%27s_Rest). As a member of the [Conclave](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Conclave), Calia traveled to the [Netherlight Temple](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Netherlight_Temple) to return the [void god](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Void_god) [Saraka](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Saraka) back to its holy form, the [naaru](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Naaru) [Saa'ra](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Saa%27ra).[21] She later became a champion to the [High Priest](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventurer).

Calia participated in the [Battle for the Exodar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Battle_for_the_Exodar) battling against [High General Rakeesh](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/High_General_Rakeesh), along with [High Priestess Ishanah](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/High_Priestess_Ishanah).

### Before the Storm

[![WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [novels](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels), [novellas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novellas), or [short stories](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Short_stories).**

“There are things you must do before that peace will be granted to you. Things that you must understand, that you must integrate into yourself. People who need your help. What one needs in order to heal will always come one's way, but sometimes it is hard to recognize it. Sometimes the most beautiful and important gifts come wrapped in pain and blood.”

- — [Saa'ra](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Saa%27ra)'s advice to Calia[22]

Following the [Argus Campaign](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Argus_Campaign), [Anduin Wrynn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) met Calia at the Netherlight Temple. After Faol introduced her as Terenas Menethil's daughter, Anduin considered her the rightful queen of Lordaeron, and was shocked and relieved to know she had survived.[18] As Anduin worked with the Conclave to heal [Azeroth](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Azeroth) from [Sargeras](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sargeras)' destruction and reconcile the [Forsaken](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken) and their human counterparts, Anduin began to see Calia as a kindred spirit and developed a bond with her similar to the one he had with [Jaina Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jaina_Proudmoore). Calia was also fond of Anduin and saw in him similarities to her beloved little brother Arthas before he became evil.

Sometimes Calia would dream of happier times, like when she sang lullabies to her infant daughter and spent time with her family. During one such dream, Calia sadly considered how her daughter would never inherit her birthright. However, her dreams frequently turned into night terrors involving the undead before she awoke from them. Usually, Calia would speak of her dreams with the [naaru](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Naaru) [Saa'ra](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Saa%27ra). Saa'ra would tell Calia that the nightmares would stop once she was ready for them to stop and that there were things she must do and become before that peace would be granted unto her. However, Saa'ra also warned that some of her trials may involve pain and blood. Though Calia admitted it didn't make her feel any better, Saa'ra told her it might once she understood that even painful developments might become hidden gifts and to trust in the purpose of her destiny. Saa'ra's insights would ease her apprehension, and she would try to repress the horrors she had endured or the memories of her daughter. Instead, she would retreat into a darkness that granted safety for wild creatures, momentary privacy for those who wanted to create, and sanctuary from her troubles. Saa'ra's warmth would then comfort her and told her to sleep in peace. As Calia left to return to her room for rest, her friend [Elinor](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Elinor) privately spoke with her to inform her of King Anduin's plans for a [Gathering](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gathering) between Forsaken and their living relatives. The conversation filled Calia with hope and purpose, making her feel as if healing had finally come her way.[22]

Anduin, [Genn Greymane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Genn_Greymane), and Calia later worked to recruit suitable human participants for the Gathering from the [Alliance](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alliance)'s side. As they worked together, Anduin asked Calia if she sought to reclaim the throne of [Lordaeron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kingdom_of_Lordaeron). When Calia asked if Anduin would aid her in reclaiming her throne even if it meant war, Anduin became conflicted. Before Anduin could respond, Calia reassured him she had no intention of reclaiming Lordaeron and the best she could do for her people in the Forsaken was to work to ensure the Gathering's success. However, she did request to participate in the Gathering. Since his body did not respond to any nefarious intentions, Anduin agreed to permit it should Faol also consent. Anduin also asked Calia if she had a child that could one day become her heir, to which Calia did acknowledge a daughter and told Anduin the tragic tale of her family. Anduin surmised the reason she identified with the Forsaken was that she hoped to be reunited with her family at the Gathering should they have been raised as undead. She later disguised herself and secretly observed the reunion between her friend Alonsus Faol, [High Exarch Turalyon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/High_Exarch_Turalyon), and King [Genn Greymane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Genn_Greymane).

Calia attended the [Gathering](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gathering) as one of the priests that were offering the participants their blessings. Disguised in a cloak and hood, she healed [Ol' Emma](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ol%27_Emma) when she fainted from heatstroke. This brought the attention of [Elsie Benton](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Vellcinda_Benton) who she had a pleasant conversation with. As the day went, [Parqual Fintallas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Parqual_Fintallas) recognized Calia beneath her hooded cloak and saw it as a reason and opportunity to defect into the Alliance. He asked Calia for a blessing and whispered to her, saying, "We need you now. It's time. You'll see. Be ready." Shortly after, Parqual, his daughter [Philia](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Philia_Fintallas), and the [Felstone](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Felstone) family began to walk towards [Stromgarde Keep](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stromgarde_Keep) — where the Alliance were stationed. As Calia Menethil saw this unfold, she revealed her true identity and took it upon herself to try to convince the other Forsaken on the field to join the Alliance, first speaking with Elsie. After being made aware of her existence, [Sylvanas Windrunner](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner) personally flew through the field and murdered Calia, who she saw as a threat to her authority. Calia's corpse was later sent back to Netherlight Temple but miraculously did not decompose. Saa'ra informed Anduin and Alonsus that they would together bring Calia back as the [Light](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Light) and she herself would have her be, and the two priests proceeded to raise the fallen Menethil back as an [undead](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Undead) touched by the Light. Calia explained to Anduin that although she was untrained in the ways of politics, as Arthas was supposed to inherit the throne, and did not intend for the Gathering to become a bloodbath nor reassert her claim to Lordaeron, she could not stand by and let a usurper like Sylvanas butcher her people out of jealousy. Seeing the Forsaken as her people, Calia had followed her heart on the field to accept them as her responsibility but now acknowledged that she was not yet ready to lead as she hadn't been the heir to the throne. For now, she would learn under Faol how to be an undead walking in the Light to better serve her people.

### Battle for Azeroth

[![Battle for Azeroth](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png/40px-BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png?25e36c)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth) **This section concerns content related to *[Battle for Azeroth](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth)*.**

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Calia_Menethil_undead.jpg/160px-Calia_Menethil_undead.jpg?53c0f9)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Calia_Menethil_undead.jpg)

During the [Fourth War](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fourth_War), a set of [Scarlet Brotherhood](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Scarlet_Brotherhood) propaganda pamphlets could be found near the [Calston Estate](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Calston_Estate) in Tirisfal. In the pamphlets, the Brotherhood made a number of spurious claims, including that [King Anduin Wrynn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) of [Stormwind](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stormwind_(kingdom)) was an [undead](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Undead)-loving traitor who worked with his "lover" [Sylvanas Windrunner](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner) to stage a fake massacre at the [Gathering](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gathering) in the [Arathi Highlands](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Arathi_Highlands). According to the Brotherhood, this was done in order to drum up sympathy for the [Forsaken](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken) and to lure out Calia so that she could be murdered and raised into undeath, allowing Anduin to marry her and usurp Lordaeron.[23][24] The pamphlets advised members of the order to rally behind the [worgen](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Worgen) king [Genn Greymane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Genn_Greymane) and help him wipe out the Forsaken, after which they would turn on Greymane and destroy the worgen as well in order to ensure that Lordaeron belongs only to "pure-blooded humans".[25] Finally, the pamphlets stated that the Brotherhood had secretly raised someone that they claimed to be the son of Princess Calia and an [Arathi](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Arathi_tribe) nobleman and therefore the rightful king of Lordaeron[26] (apparently ignoring the fact that Calia's actual child was a daughter with a common footman, not a son with a nobleman).[3] The Brotherhood promised that they would reveal this so-called Menethil heir and place him on the throne after the undead and worgen have been wiped from the land, thereby restoring the [Menethil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/House_Menethil) dynasty.[26]

Following the [Battle of Dazar'alor](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Battle_of_Dazar%27alor), Jaina Proudmoore told Anduin that she had heard rumors about Calia and asked the king to tell her what happened in Arathi.[27] After [Baine Bloodhoof](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Baine_Bloodhoof) reunited the recently resurrected [Derek Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Derek_Proudmoore) with his sister, Jaina brought Derek to Calia in the hopes that she could help him.[28]

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Calia_and_the_Proudmoore_Siblings.jpg/320px-Calia_and_the_Proudmoore_Siblings.jpg?8d88e0)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Calia_and_the_Proudmoore_Siblings.jpg)

In time, [Varok Saurfang](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Varok_Saurfang)'s revolutionaries clashed with Sylvanas's loyalists, which saw the conflict ending in Saurfang's death and Sylvanas's abandonment of the Horde. In the aftermath, Jaina met with Calia and Derek at [Seabreeze Village](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Seabreeze_Village) in [Stormsong Valley](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stormsong_Valley). During this meeting, Calia and Derek learned that the war was over, and it was implied that Calia and Derek had grown close. As the three conversed, the Proudmoore siblings remarked that [others](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken) would benefit from Calia's guidance, much like Derek had.[29] Unbeknownst to the three, the [Speaker of the Horde](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventurer) was spying on their meeting at the behest of [Lilian Voss](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lilian_Voss), who after being informed of the details remarked that she must act quickly.[30]

Around the time of the [Black Empire](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Black_Empire)'s assault on Azeroth, Calia received a letter from Lilian asking her to help the Forsaken in the wake of their abandonment by Sylvanas. Calia agreed as she wanted to help her people and honor her father and the name Menethil. In another meeting in Stormsong Valley, she informed Jaina and Derek of her decision to leave [Kul Tiras](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kul_Tiras). To her surprise, Derek decided to accompany her. Jaina accepted Calia and Derek's decisions but insisted on first bringing them to [Katherine](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Katherine_Proudmoore) and [Tandred](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tandred_Proudmoore) so they could get a proper farewell.[31] Calia and Derek traveled to the [Calston Estate](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Calston_Estate) in the [Tirisfal Glades](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tirisfal_Glades), where Lilian apologized for her role in Derek's rebirth and introduced the two to [Delaryn Summermoon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Delaryn_Summermoon) and the other Forsaken [night elves](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Night_elf), who had likewise been abandoned by Sylvanas. Calia and Derek quickly offered to help the undead elves and subsequently left the estate with them,[32] vowing to help them find a future rooted in something beyond hatred and malice.[33]

### Shadows Rising

[![WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [novels](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels), [novellas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novellas), or [short stories](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Short_stories).**

At some point after the fall of [N'Zoth](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/N%27Zoth), Calia joined the [Horde](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Horde) and became a councilor of the [Forsaken](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken).

Knowing that the Forsaken couldn't remain in [Orgrimmar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Orgrimmar) forever, Voss and Calia sought a new home for their people, while believing that the ruins of Lordaeron could one day be reclaimed and their homes there restored. Despite the truce between the Horde and Alliance, they knew that the Alliance ships wouldn't be happy to see Horde flags at sea, though Calia was confident that Derek could intervene on their behalf if they were intercepted. Calia then attended a [Horde Council](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Horde_Council) meeting where [Thrall](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thrall) noticed that she appeared to be watching Voss's every move, as if she might be tested on it later. When Calia and Voss explained why they had been late to said meeting, she brought up her belief about Derek causing [Queen Talanji](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Talanji) of the [Zandalari Empire](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Zandalari_Empire) to declare that [House Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/House_Proudmoore) could do nothing for them as she blamed them for the death of her father, [King Rastakhan](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/King_Rastakhan) and objected to peace with Jaina. However, Voss was quick to remind her that Derek was one of the Forsaken and that the Forsaken were the Horde. The meeting was soon adjourned.

After the [Earthen Ring](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Earthen_Ring) shaman [Yukha](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Yukha) warned Thrall that something was terribly wrong in the spirit world, Thrall made the decision to go to [Nordrassil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nordrassil) and decided to bring [Baine Bloodhoof](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Baine_Bloodhoof) and Calia, due to her desire to bridge the divide between the Forsaken and the [kaldorei](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Night_elf) who were raised into undeath, with him. She was struck by the beauty of Nordrassil and considered it a place of mourning after sensing the darkness over the place. Upon meeting with [Malfurion Stormrage](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Malfurion_Stormrage), [Tyrande Whisperwind](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tyrande_Whisperwind), [Shandris Feathermoon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shandris_Feathermoon), and [Maiev Shadowsong](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Maiev_Shadowsong), Calia found herself shifting nervously in the face of their frosty, rigid silence. Thrall subsequently formally apologized to the night elves and cited Calia as an example of how the Horde hoped to change. Nodding silently by Thrall's side, she listened as he revealed to them how Voss now spoke for the Forsaken and how both women sought to reforge themselves anew, free of Sylvanas, free of her poisonous influence.

When the meeting started to dissolve into an argument about [responsibility](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/War_of_the_Thorns) for [Teldrassil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Teldrassil), Calia voiced that these disagreements were a distraction and that the divided sides only kept them from apprehending the one who gave the order. In the aftermath of Tyrande's revelation that she saw their words as empty pledges of justice, Calia, along with Baine, allowed herself to be corralled out of the area by Yukha. However, Thrall refused to be corralled out and promised Tyrande that he would deliver what is owed: Sylvanas's head. The group subsequently departed Nordrassil and arrived in [Orgrimmar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Orgrimmar) soon after.[34]

### Death Rising

[![Shadowlands](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Shadowlands-Icon-Inline.png/48px-Shadowlands-Icon-Inline.png?d84a63)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Shadowlands) **This section concerns content related to *[Shadowlands](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Shadowlands)*.**

Calia joined the [Horde Council](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Horde_Council) in convening atop of [Grommash Hold](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Grommash_Hold) following [the return of the Scourge](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Death_Rising) and the abduction of [Thrall](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thrall) and [Baine Bloodhoof](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Baine_Bloodhoof). She soon learned from [Valeera Sanguinar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Valeera_Sanguinar) that [Jaina Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jaina_Proudmoore) and [Anduin Wrynn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) had also been abducted, and that an attempt was made on [Tyrande Whisperwind](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tyrande_Whisperwind), who neutralized her attackers.[35] She reflected on how she thought she understood the Forsaken when she was among the living, only to truly see the depths of what they have endured after her resurrection. She further revealed that with the help of [Lilian Voss](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lilian_Voss), she found ways to help those who felt abandoned and alone. She then declared that all of Azeroth trembled beneath the specter of death. And fear, that if left unchecked will consume every soul it touches. She thus vowed not to allow that to happen, not to the people of [Lordaeron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lordaeron), nor to any who fight for the good of [Azeroth](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Azeroth).

After [Lor'themar Theron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lor%27themar_Theron) expressed satisfaction at the death of [Nathanos Blightcaller](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nathanos_Blightcaller) and hope that [Tyrande Whisperwind](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tyrande_Whisperwind)'s rage remained fixed upon [Sylvanas Windrunner](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner), Calia reminded him that she had given counsel to the kaldorei who were raised into undeath after the battle for Darkshore. She further explained how she hoped giving them a path forward and sharing this knowledge with Tyrande, would help ease her spirits, if only a little. In response, Lor'themar revealed that [Lilian Voss](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lilian_Voss) had informed him of her efforts and hoped that fate would offer her the chance to see her kind offer bear fruit.[36]

During this time, she spoke with [Valeera Sanguinar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Valeera_Sanguinar) and affirmed a friendship with her.

### Shadowlands

[![Shadowlands](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Shadowlands-Icon-Inline.png/48px-Shadowlands-Icon-Inline.png?d84a63)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Shadowlands) **This section concerns content related to *[Shadowlands](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Shadowlands)*.**

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Horde_Council_Frozen_Throne.jpg/320px-Horde_Council_Frozen_Throne.jpg?ad1fef)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Horde_Council_Frozen_Throne.jpg)

Calia joined the council in a meeting with the leaders of the Alliance and [Bolvar Fordragon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Bolvar_Fordragon) at the [Frozen Throne](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Frozen_Throne) within [Icecrown Citadel](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Icecrown_Citadel). When Lor'themar voiced how they couldn't afford to go on what might well be a suicide mission to rescue their allies for the stability of Azeroth, [Tyrande Whisperwind](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tyrande_Whisperwind) grew hostile. Though prepared to battle alongside the Horde, Calia reminded Tyrande that the [kaldorei](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kaldorei) needed her in order to have any hope of finding peace. However, Tyrande was unmoved and was outraged that Calia spoke of peace, in light of the [atrocities](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Burning_of_Teldrassil) committed by the Forsaken during the [Fourth War](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fourth_War). Ultimately, the Horde and Alliance stood down when Bolvar reminded them that they could not afford another conflict in Azeroth.[37] Calia then witnessed the ritual to open the doorway to the [Maw](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Maw) and watched as [champions](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventurer) of Azeroth, the [Knights of the Ebon Blade](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Knights_of_the_Ebon_Blade), and even Tyrande Whisperwind enter to rescue their comrades and seek out Sylvanas.[38]

Calia later traveled with [Taelia Fordragon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Taelia_Fordragon) to [Oribos](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Oribos) in the center of the Shadowlands to meet up with Bolvar there. The pair pleaded for Bolvar to use the [Helm of Domination](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Helm_of_Domination) to seek out their allies who had been captured by [Sylvanas Windrunner](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner) within the [Maw](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Maw). Despite his initial reluctance, as such attempts seemed to be costly and dangerous, Bolvar was eventually moved by his daughter's repeated pleas, and he agreed to honor the request of his "Shining Star".[39] While Talia was chilled by the cries of [Jaina Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jaina_Proudmoore) and [Thrall](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thrall), Calia focused on how they hadn't heard [Anduin Wrynn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) or [Baine Bloodhoof](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Baine_Bloodhoof), and thus asked if Bolvar could try again to find them. However, Bolvar revealed that there was a price to be paid for such visions, and he dared not attempt another so soon.[40]

Following Baine's and Thrall's recovery from [Torghast, Tower of the Damned](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Torghast,_Tower_of_the_Damned), Calia sent word to [Aggra](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Aggra) and [Lor'themar Theron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lor%27themar_Theron).[41] After discovering that Sylvanas and [the Jailer](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Jailer) intend to turn Anduin into a weapon, Calia stated that the Light is strong with Anduin and believed the Light would help him see things through.[42]

Following the defeat of [Sire Denathrius](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sire_Denathrius), an ally of the Jailer, Bolvar felt a darkness calling to him within Torghast.[43] Though Calia was worried over the effects of peering within Torghast would have on him, Bolvar declared that they must know the truth. She subsequently learned that a new mourneblade was being forged within the tower and that the Jailer decided that the time had come for Anduin to become his agent, regardless of his choice.[44] When the Jailer attempted to gain control of Bolvar, Calia learned that the [Lich King](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lich_King) was meant to herald his coming.[45] As a result of this, while Calia still acknowledged that her brother had made his choices, she felt that the Jailer had a hand in them too.

#### Eternity's End

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/A_Long_Walk_-_Calia_and_Lilian.jpg/240px-A_Long_Walk_-_Calia_and_Lilian.jpg?74d1f6)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:A_Long_Walk_-_Calia_and_Lilian.jpg)

After the Jailer's defeat, Calia attended Sylvanas's trial in the [Crucible](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Crucible_(Oribos)) alongside a visiting Lilian Voss.[46]

Sometime later she joined [Forsaken](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken) in mustering in the ruins of the [kingdom of Lordaeron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kingdom_of_Lordaeron), as they sought to reclaim as much as they could of the [Tirisfal Glades](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tirisfal_Glades). Thus she called Horde champions to aid and meet her at [Brill](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Brill), while also accepting the aid of Alliance champions who had taken a disguise to blend in with the Forsaken.[47][48] However she received a mixed response, as while the likes of [Dark Ranger Velonara](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dark_Ranger_Velonara) were willing to give her a chance, [Deathstalker Commander Belmont](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Deathstalker_Commander_Belmont) was both distrustful and skeptical about her. For her own part, Calia accepted that she wouldn't be universally accepted because of Arthas' deeds or perceptions about her seeking leadership or advocating the Alliance's interests. She merely vowed to help the Forsaken in any way she could, thus she called upon a number of influential figures that had risen to prominence among the Forsaken, in order to meet and discuss the path forward.[49]

While Voss wanted to directly focus on dealing with the plague in the [Undercity](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Undercity), Belmont questioned what they should do about Calia, only for [Dark Ranger Velonara](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dark_Ranger_Velonara) to declare that they should focus on the blight. She then turned to [Master Apothecary Faranell](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Master_Apothecary_Faranell) and questioned if he had more plague eaters, only for him to reveal to Calia and the others that he only had one left, and that while he could make more for them to be effective, they must be formulated to survive the plague. This in turn inspired Calia to point out that they might be able to turn to the plague-lords of [Maldraxxus](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Maldraxxus) for insight. When Faranell revealed that he required an undiluted sample of the plague from deep within its reaches, Calia vowed to acquire it for him, but both Faranell and Belmont were skeptical of her being able to acquire it and survive.[50]

Calia then called upon the light and with the champion by her side, she ventured into the plague and successfully acquired the plague sample, which greatly surprised Belmont.[51] With the sample in their possession, Calia, Voss, the champion, and the plague eater went to Maldraxxus, where they met with [Margrave Sin'dane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Margrave_Sin%27dane).[52]

After Sin'dane directed the group to meet with [Plague Deviser Marileth](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Plague_Deviser_Marileth) at the [House of Plagues](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/House_of_Plagues), Calia questioned Sin'dane about the nature of undeath, and revealed how while the Forsaken were raised through necromancy, which provided them a shared origin, and sense of unity, she had been raised by the light. She further remarked that this difference made her question if she could ever truly find a place among them. In response, Sin'dane revealed that regardless of any magic, necromancy is necromancy, that Calia perceived a difference when there was none. Calia subsequently thanked the Margrave for her aid before leaving to seek out Marileth.[53] As they worked together to empower the plague eater, Calia called upon the light to shield them from the caustic fog.[54] She also directed them with slaying creatures within the House of Plagues to help the plague eater to help it gain fortitude.[55] Near the end, the blight turned into a living manifestation known as the [Lordaeron Blight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lordaeron_Blight), which was quickly slain, and proved the plague eater could aid them in reclaiming the [Ruins of Lordaeron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ruins_of_Lordaeron).[56] With this accomplished, Calia and the others returned to Tirisfal Glades to deliver the plague eater to the apothecaries, where Calia informed Faranell of how the blight sample became a living manifestation, to which Faranell was quick to declare it ingenious, as it would be easier to kill.[57]

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Desolate_Council.jpg/320px-Desolate_Council.jpg?c7e9d3)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Desolate_Council.jpg)

This plan was ultimately successful as the plague manifested as an [Amalgam](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Amalgam) and was slain, liberating the Ruins of Lordaeron from the plague.[58] Following this, Voss declared that the Forsaken have many points of view and should be led by a council, not a single ruler, a motion accepted by the other Forsaken leaders. While Belmont had his reservations about having a Menethil on the council, Calia was quick to declare that she had no desire for a throne, that her place was amongst them, with a vow to serve all their people. Thus, a new [Desolate Council](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Desolate_Council) composed of Voss, Calia, Belmont, Faranell, and Velonara was created to lead the Forsaken.[59] With the council's formation, Calia journeyed into the [Lordaeron Throne Room](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Imperial_chamber) room where she reflected on the past and the tragedy of her family, of how she was all that remained of the Menethil line, before being comforted by Voss, who talked about how the Forsaken were their home. However, Calia questioned if the other Forsaken would truly ever accept her, to which Voss admitted that while it was a long road, they could use her help, which gave Calia the encouragement she needed to step forward.[60]

Following this, Calia styled herself as The Pallid Lady, officially recognized [Derek Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Derek_Proudmoore) as her champion, and promised the Alliance heroes who had aided the Forsaken that one of the first topics that she planned on proposing to her fellow council members was the withdrawal of Forsaken troops from the [Kingdom of Gilneas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)).[61]

The Forsaken spoke with [Taelia Fordragon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Taelia_Fordragon) and [Wrathion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Wrathion) during the reception. ### Dragonflight

[![Dragonflight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dragonflight-Icon-Inline.png/48px-Dragonflight-Icon-Inline.png?be4c0e)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight) **This section concerns content related to *[Dragonflight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight)*.**

After the [Scarlet Crusade](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Scarlet_Crusade) forces led by [Scarlet Commander Forsythe](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Scarlet_Commander_Forsythe) seized control of [Fenris Isle](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fenris_Isle), they used it as a staging ground to strike against the [Forsaken](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken) within the [Ruins of Lordaeron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ruins_of_Lordaeron). As a part of the Forsaken counterattack, she directed Forsaken [champions](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventurer) to kill [Lieutenant Hawlsey](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lieutenant_Hawlsey) and obtain [their armor](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hawlsey%27s_Armor) in order to spy on the crusade.[63] Following the crusade's defeat and the death of Commander Forsythe, Calia returned to the Ruins of Lordaeron where she joined the rest of the [Desolate Council](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Desolate_Council) in declaring that the champion represented what it meant to be Forsaken.[64]

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/To_Gilneas.jpg/320px-To_Gilneas.jpg?57fa42)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:To_Gilneas.jpg)

Calia and Voss called upon Horde champions to meet them within [Pyrewood Village](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Pyrewood_Village) for a clandestine operation to help the [Gilneans](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)) reclaim [Gilneas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gilneas) from the [Scarlet Crusade](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Scarlet_Crusade). Working with [Tess Greymane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tess_Greymane), the trio devised a plan in which the Forsaken army would serve as a distraction for the Crusade, while a small strike team sneaked into the city and opened the gates for the Gilneas army. Though initially outraged at the Forsaken presence, [Genn Greymane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Genn_Greymane) gave command of the operation to Tess, which allowed for the plan to move forward.[65][66] While Voss joined the strike team, Calia gathered the Forsaken forces.[67] As the Forsaken and [Alliance](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alliance) forces clashed against the Crusade, Calia joined the final battle against their leader, [Inquisitor Fairbell](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Inquisitor_Fairbell).[68]

As the Gilneans went about reclaiming their home, the Forsaken departed from the area, though not before Calia learned that Genn abdicated his throne in favor of Tess.[69] While unable to hope for peace, Calia hoped that their efforts to help led to an understanding of the plights of both Gilneans and the Forsaken. Regrouping at Pyrewood Village, Calia formally thanked Horde champions for their assistance and expressed hope that Tess would continue to be more diplomatic than her father. In response, Voss revealed that she knew that Tess would as the new Queen cared more about what people do than what they are. With the mission concluded, the pair returned to the Ruins of Lordaeron.[70]
