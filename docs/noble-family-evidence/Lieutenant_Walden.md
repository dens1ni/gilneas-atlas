**Lieutenant Walden** is a [human](/wiki/Human)[quest giver](/wiki/Quest_giver) located in [Merchant Square](/wiki/Merchant_Square) in the [worgen](/wiki/Worgen) starting zone of [Gilneas City](/wiki/Gilneas_City_(starting_zone)). He was under [Liam Greymane](/wiki/Liam_Greymane)'s command to help evacuate the city, but was killed by [worgen](/wiki/Worgen) during their invasion of Gilneas.
