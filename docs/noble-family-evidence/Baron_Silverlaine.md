**Baron Silverlaine** is a [human](/wiki/Human) [ghost](/wiki/Ghost) and the second boss in [Shadowfang Keep](/wiki/Shadowfang_Keep). He is located in the Dining Hall, or Great Hall via teleport.

Baron Silveraline and his family were killed by the [worgen](/wiki/Worgen), summoned by [Archmage Arugal](/wiki/Archmage_Arugal) from the [Emerald Dream](/wiki/Emerald_Dream) to aid [Gilneans](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)) against the [Scourge](/wiki/Scourge), after they turned on the Gilneans. After the [Fourth War](/wiki/Fourth_War), [Mathias Shaw](/wiki/Mathias_Shaw) reported a common belief that the ghost of Baron Silverlaine still haunts the keep.[1]

## Background[]

### Adventure Guide[]

*Baron Silverlaine was master of the keep before it fell to Arugal's worgen. Now his cursed spirit calls upon the very wolf-beasts that slaughtered him to punish further trespassers into his home.*

## Attacks and abilities[]

- [**Veil of Shadow**](https://www.wowhead.com/?spell=23224) — Baron Silverlaine curses his current enemy target, reducing magical healing effects done to the target by 50% for 8 sec.
- [**Cursed Veil**](https://www.wowhead.com/?spell=93956) — The Cursed Veil inflicts 24375 to 25625 Shadow damage and reduces healing effects by 75% for 8 sec.
- [**Summon Worgen Spirit**](https://www.wowhead.com/?spell=93857) — Baron Silverlaine summons an ancient Worgen Spirit to his aid! Dependant on Silverlaine's HP level, cast at 70% and 35% on normal, and 90%, 60% and 30% on heroic.

### [Odo the Blindwatcher](/wiki/Odo_the_Blindwatcher)[]

- **Howling Rage** — Odo the Blindwatcher increases his Physical damage dealt by 10% and size by 15% for 20 sec. This effect stacks up to 5 times.
- **Blinding Shadows** — Odo the Blindwatcher inflicts 73 to 76 Shadow damage every second for 6 sec

### [Razorclaw the Butcher](/wiki/Razorclaw_the_Butcher)[]

- **Spectral Rush** — Razorclaw leaps at the furthest away enemy target, stunning and inflicting 38 to 42 Physical damage every 0.5 second for 3 sec.
- **Butcher Drain** — Whenever Razorclaw inflicts damage with a melee attack, an additional 24 to 25 health is drained from the target.

### [Rethilgore](/wiki/Rethilgore)[]

**Soul Drain** — Rethilgore channels on a random enemy target, immobilizing and draining 25 health every 1 seconds for 4 sec. Rethilgore heals for twice the amount of health drained.

### [Wolf Master Nandos](/wiki/Wolf_Master_Nandos)[]

- **Claw** — Nandos rakes his current target, dealing 125% of his normal melee attack in Physical damage.
- **Summon Lupine Spirit** — When Wolf Master Nandos enters combat, he summons three Lupine Spectres to fight at his side for 30 sec.
  - **[Lupine Spectre](/wiki/Lupine_Spectre)** — This creature has low health.

## Strategy[]

Silverlaine will periodically cast Cursed Veil; the magic effect should be dispelled if the affected targets are going to be healed in the next 8 seconds.

At each 25% health, Silverlaine will summon a random worgen spirit; those adds should be tanked and killed before resuming DPS on Silverlaine.
