**Lord Walden** was a [human](/wiki/Human) nobleman from eastern [Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_peninsula).

## History[]

### Curse of the Worgen[]

**This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [manga](/wiki/Manga) or [comics](/wiki/Comics).**

One day before the attack on [Gilneas City](/wiki/Gilneas_City), he quarreled with [Baron Ashbury](/wiki/Baron_Ashbury) because of the bad situation in Gilneas.

### Cataclysm[]

**This section concerns content related to *[Cataclysm](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm)*.**

He joined with Lord [Vincent Godfrey](/wiki/Vincent_Godfrey) to turn [Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane) over to the [Forsaken](/wiki/Forsaken) in exchange for an end to hostilities. Along with his ally, [Baron Ashbury](/wiki/Baron_Ashbury), Walden was slain by Gilnean [worgen](/wiki/Worgen) who fought to save their King. Godfrey, Ashbury and Walden were all raised in undeath by the Forsaken and served them for a time. After Godfrey betrayed the Forsaken by slaying [Sylvanas Windrunner](/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner), Walden fled with his master into nearby [Shadowfang Keep](/wiki/Shadowfang_Keep).

## Notes and trivia[]

- When he was alive, Lord Walden wore [[Lord Walden's Top Hat]](/wiki/Lord_Walden%27s_Top_Hat) and [[Banded Girdle]](/wiki/Banded_Girdle), as well as slightly altered versions of [[Nobleman's Coat]](/wiki/Nobleman%27s_Coat), [[Nobleman's Pantaloons]](/wiki/Nobleman%27s_Pantaloons), and [[Noble's Fancy Boots]](/wiki/Noble%27s_Fancy_Boots).
- After being raised into undeath, he began to wear a unique outfit resembling the Fireweave cloth set.
