**Dame Alys Finnsson** is a [worgen](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Worgen), affiliated with the [Twilight's Hammer](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight%27s_Hammer), found in the [Twilight Highlands](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Highlands).

## Abilities[]

- [**Faerie Fire**](https://www.wowhead.com/?spell=25602) — Reduces an enemy's armor for 20 sec. While affected, the target cannot use stealth or invisibility.
- [**Insect Swarm**](https://www.wowhead.com/?spell=83017) — The enemy target is swarmed by insects, decreasing their chance to hit with melee and ranged attacks by 3% and causing Nature damage over 12 sec.
- [**Starfire**](https://www.wowhead.com/?spell=78907) — Causes Arcane damage to the target.
