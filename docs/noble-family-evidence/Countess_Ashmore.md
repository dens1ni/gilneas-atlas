**Countess Ashmore** is a [blue dragon](/wiki/Blue_dragon)[1] featured in the *[The Witchwood](/wiki/Hearthstone:_The_Witchwood)* expansion for *[Hearthstone](/wiki/Hearthstone_(game))*.

The countess is an enigma, and no one in [Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_peninsula) seems to have heard of her noble line prior to the Witchwood curse, but suddenly, the records show an Ashmore family tree stretching all the way back to pre-[Alliance](/wiki/Alliance_of_Lordaeron) days, written in a hand none of the royal scribes recognize. She's taken up residence in an old manor that's been abandoned since before the first battle of Gilneas, but while some claim that many voices can be heard emerging from the old estate, she's the only one who's been seen entering or leaving. It's rumored that Ashmore is researching the curse, and she has been asking questions of those who claim to have witnessed dragons in the wood. It is not clear if Ashmore is truly the scion of a forgotten noble family returned home in a time of need, or if she is something much more dangerous.[2]
