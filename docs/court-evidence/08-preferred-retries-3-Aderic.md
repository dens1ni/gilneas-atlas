**Aderic** was a [human](/wiki/Human) who was a [Gilnean](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)) patriarch. He is buried at [Aderic's Repose](/wiki/Aderic%27s_Repose) at the top of the hill. When the Gilneans retreated from [Gilneas City](/wiki/Gilneas_City) through the [Undertaker's Pass](/wiki/Undertaker%27s_Pass) and into the Repose, they disturbed the graves of their ancestors, forcing their spirits to rise from their graves. [Krennan Aranas](/wiki/Krennan_Aranas) sought his blessing by offering and placing the [Blessed Offerings](/wiki/Blessed_Offerings) on his grave in hopes of calming the risen Gilnean spirits. He appears to have accepted the offer as the spirits returned to the grave.[1]
