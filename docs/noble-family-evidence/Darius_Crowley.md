**Lord Darius Crowley** was once a [Gilnean](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)) nobleman. A pro-[Alliance](/wiki/Alliance_of_Lordaeron) figure, he dissented with King [Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane)'s decision to separate his people with the [Greymane Wall](/wiki/Greymane_Wall). Crowley led the [Northgate Rebellion](/wiki/Northgate_Rebellion) that caused a civil war in [Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_peninsula), during which he was imprisoned. When Gilneas was attacked by [worgen](/wiki/Worgen), he was freed and became a worgen himself. Both Crowley and Greymane reconciled and joined forces against the threats Gilneas faced. He now leads the [Gilneas Liberation Front](/wiki/Gilneas_Liberation_Front) which fights against the Forsaken along with the [Bloodfang Pack](/wiki/Bloodfang_Pack) and the [7th Legion](/wiki/7th_Legion). When the [Burning Legion](/wiki/Burning_Legion) returned to assault Azeroth, Darius and his daughter [Lorna](/wiki/Lorna_Crowley) joined Genn on the *[Skyfire](/wiki/The_Skyfire)* as part of his army forces.

## Biography[]

### Lord of His Pack[]

At the time of the [Second War](/wiki/Second_War), Lord Crowley was a nobleman whose lands and people were in northern [Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)), including some land outside of the border that would later be known as the [Greymane Wall](/wiki/Greymane_Wall). He owned the towns of [Pyrewood Village](/wiki/Pyrewood_Village) and [Ambermill](/wiki/Ambermill). Despite being young and new to the Gilnean council of Lords, his altruistic and charismatic qualities made him loved by the Gilnean people and earned him the respect and friendship of [King Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane). Crowley was one of the nobles who accompanied Greymane to [Capital City](/wiki/Capital_City) to hear of [Stormwind](/wiki/Stormwind_(kingdom))'s fall and [Anduin Lothar](/wiki/Anduin_Lothar) warning about the invading [Orcish Horde](/wiki/Orcish_Horde). Crowley was sympathetic to the Alliance's plight and advised Greymane to send aid to the newly-formed [Alliance](/wiki/Alliance_of_Lordaeron). Greymane agreed out of self-serving reasons but initially offered only token support.[1]

Years later, the following the Horde's defeat, various disputes arose among the leaders of the Alliance. Greymane resented the increased taxes to help rebuild Stormwind and maintain the orcish [internment camps](/wiki/Internment_camp). The king began to believe seceding from the Alliance would be more beneficial to Gilneas. After [Quel'Thalas](/wiki/Quel%27Thalas_(kingdom)) departed from the Alliance, both Gilneas and [Stromgarde](/wiki/Stromgarde_(kingdom)) withdrew as well.[2] When Genn avowed to wall his kingdom off from the rest of the world, he decided a mountainous region cutting through some of Crowley's lands would be the best location for the Greymane Wall, at the suggestion of [Lord Vincent Godfrey](/wiki/Lord_Vincent_Godfrey). Greymane hoped that Crowley would understand his reasoning.[1] In retaliation for Greymane's decision, Crowley was responsible for sending the [Gilneas Brigade](/wiki/Gilneas_Brigade_(Warcraft_III)) to [Jaina Proudmoore](/wiki/Jaina_Proudmoore) and the [Human Expedition](/wiki/Human_Expedition) during the [Third War](/wiki/Third_War).[1]

The King had tried to reason with Crowley, to make it clear that this wall was the way forward, and to explain why assisting the Alliance was so wrong. But Crowley refused, and insisted that he was doing what was best for the future of Gilneas, that he would end the King's "tyranny", and started a rebellion against Greymane, gathering like-minded Gilneans including his daughter [Lorna](/wiki/Lorna_Crowley) and [Tobias Mistmantle](/wiki/Tobias_Mistmantle) in forming the [Northgate rebellion](/wiki/Northgate_rebellion). The resulting civil war weakened the kingdom and split the populace into two groups, "royalist" and "rebel", labels that continued to spark fights years after the event ended. He was eventually arrested on the grounds of treason and held at [Stoneward Prison](/wiki/Stoneward_Prison).

### Cataclysm[]

**This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [manga](/wiki/Manga) or [comics](/wiki/Comics).**

**This section concerns content related to *[Cataclysm](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm)*.**

Shortly before the attack on Gilneas City, Genn was thinking about granting Crowley amnesty.[3]

When the capital of Gilneas was attacked by feral [worgen](/wiki/Worgen), Crowley was released by Gilnean [adventurers](/wiki/Adventurer) and convinced to give aid to Greymane, who had ordered the evacuation of Gilneas. When he and his released men regrouped, he gave them a speech of a union against the worgen. In order to divert the worgen's attention from the fleeing citizens, Crowley led a group of brave Gilneans to stand against them at [Light's Dawn Cathedral](/wiki/Light%27s_Dawn_Cathedral). Crowley and his followers were overwhelmed, and [became worgen](/wiki/Worgen_curse) themselves.[4]

When [Belysra](/wiki/Belysra) took [Halford Ramsey](/wiki/Halford_Ramsey) to [Tal'doren](/wiki/Tal%27doren), he encountered Darius Crowley, one of many Gilneans that Belysra and her night elves had been trying to help with their worgen affliction. A Loyalist, Halford still thought of Darius as a rebel and a traitor and lunged at him but the fight was broken up by Belysra who reminded them that the past didn't matter anymore and that they both needed to put it behind them in order to preserve the future of Gilneas. Both agreed to put the past behind them and work together to capture feral worgen in the woods of the [Blackwald](/wiki/Blackwald) and make them sane again and regain their humanity.[5]

He worked with the adventurer to retrieve the stolen [Scythe of Elune](/wiki/Scythe_of_Elune) from the hands of the Forsaken. He was worried of how Lorna would react to the news that he had the curse, but she was fine with it (considering that she had thought he had been killed instead). As the [Forsaken invasion](/wiki/Invasion_of_Gilneas) went on, [Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane) and Lord Godfrey traveled to [Tal'doren](/wiki/Tal%27doren) to ask Crowley to help unite Gilneas against the [Forsaken](/wiki/Forsaken). Due to [Lord Godfrey](/wiki/Lord_Godfrey)'s prejudices towards the worgen, Darius was hesitant to accept. Greymane tells Darius that he is asking as an equal and after revealing that he too had succumbed to the [Worgen curse](/wiki/Worgen_curse), Darius agreed to join forces.[6]

Halford Ramsey along with Lord Crowley and his Gilnean worgen followed [Belrysa Starbreeze](/wiki/Belrysa_Starbreeze) in confronting [Alpha Prime](/wiki/Alpha_Prime) at [Tal'doren](/wiki/Tal%27doren). Belrysa attempted to use the [Scythe of Elune](/wiki/Scythe_of_Elune) to seal away Alpha Prime and his [Wolf Cult](/wiki/Wolf_Cult) beneath [Daral'nir](/wiki/Daral%27nir) in the [Emerald Dream](/wiki/Emerald_Dream), but before she could do so, a [Forsaken](/wiki/Forsaken) [dark ranger](/wiki/Dark_ranger) shot her in the arm. This caused her to drop the [Scythe of Elune](/wiki/Scythe_of_Elune), and Alpha Prime signaled for the army of Forsaken to surround them. Alpha Prime took possession of the scythe, but just as he was about to finish Belysra off with it, the [spirit wolf](/wiki/Spirit_wolf) of Belysra's old lover — [Arvell](/wiki/Arvell) — killed Alpha Prime. With the death of their leader, the Wolf Cult scattered; leaving the Forsaken without their lupine allies and open to attack from the night elves and Gilneans. Shortly afterwards he, Halford and other Gilneans witnessed King Genn Greymane's confession of being afflicted with the [worgen curse](/wiki/Worgen_curse) to his subjects and the Gilnean people's acceptance of him.[5]

Darius later participated in [1-30] [The Battle for Gilneas City](/wiki/The_Battle_for_Gilneas_City) with Genn and though they were successful in retaking the city, the Forsaken's Horde reinforcements and intent on using the [Forsaken Blight](/wiki/Forsaken_Blight) forced the Gilneans to flee and seek sanctuary in [Darnassus](/wiki/Darnassus). Before the worgen left, he was present at Liam's funeral in [Aderic's Repose](/wiki/Aderic%27s_Repose) and in Keel Harbor he led the organization of defenses against the invading Horde. While most of the worgen left, he and his daughter remained.

### Gilneas Liberation Front[]

**This section concerns content related to *[Cataclysm](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm)*.**

With Gilneas now in enemy hands following the [Forsaken invasion](/wiki/Invasion_of_Gilneas), Lord Crowley led the [Gilneas Liberation Front](/wiki/Gilneas_Liberation_Front) against the [Forsaken](/wiki/Forsaken) in [Silverpine Forest](/wiki/Silverpine_Forest). With the [Bloodfang pack](/wiki/Bloodfang_pack) and the [Hillsbrad Worgen](/wiki/Hillsbrad_Worgen) bolstering their numbers, and with help from the [7th Legion](/wiki/7th_Legion), who used a submarine to pass through enemy lines, the [Alliance](/wiki/Alliance) gained the upper hand in Gilneas. However, agents of the Horde ended up destroying some of the reinforcements and enabled the Forsaken to resurrect Lord Godfrey as one of their own,[7] a fact that none of the Gilneans were aware of.

With Godfrey's help the Forsaken successfully captured Darius's daughter [Lorna](/wiki/Lorna_Crowley).[8] Crowley led the Alliance's final push into [Silverpine Forest](/wiki/Silverpine_Forest). [Sylvanas Windrunner](/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner) herself rode out to meet with Darius at the shattered [Greymane Wall](/wiki/Greymane_Wall) and gave him two choices - the surrender of the Gilneas Liberation Front, or Lorna's death and resurrection as one of the Forsaken. Without a moment of hesitation, Darius agreed to surrender the Gilneas City in order to protect his daughter.[9] Sylvanas allowed them to retreat to Gilneas,[10] which was still contested between the Alliance and the Horde.

### Legion[]

**This section concerns content related to *[Legion](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion)*.**

Following the [Battle for the Broken Shore](/wiki/Battle_for_the_Broken_Shore), Darius and his daughter Lorna joined [Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane) on the *[Skyfire](/wiki/Skyfire)*. He is seen standing next to [Gilnean Footmen](/wiki/Gilnean_Footman_(Stormwind_City)).

The [Gilneas Brigade](/wiki/Gilneas_Brigade) led by Genn and Darius' daughter Lorna established [Greywatch](/wiki/Greywatch) to secure [Stormheim](/wiki/Stormheim) from threats. Darius later made an appearance if an Alliance player reaches Prestige Rank 2, where he is found in Stormwind Keep alongside Princess [Tess Greymane](/wiki/Tess_Greymane) following the accepting of the quest, [A Royal Audience](/wiki/A_Royal_Audience). He was among the audience that congratulated the character for their battles against the Horde, following their receiving of the [[Grand Marshal's Medal of Valor]](/wiki/Grand_Marshal%27s_Medal_of_Valor) from Anduin Wrynn, as well as an artifact appearance.

Crowley was later found to have been taken captive by the naga on the [Broken Shore](/wiki/Broken_Shore) alongside many other warriors. An Alliance warrior freed him, and he aided them in freeing the other warriors from the front. His counterpart for the Horde is [Eitrigg](/wiki/Eitrigg). Notably, unlike Eitrigg who does not have this function, if the warrior frees any Forsaken, Crowley has a chance to run up and instantly kill them. If a pack of warriors is freed and he does this, any worgen freed will also cheer.

Crowley then joined the [Valarjar](/wiki/Valarjar) as one of their followers, which also opened up recruitment for the [7th Legion](/wiki/7th_Legion); despite Crowley not being a part of this organization. It's possible this is just due to his place as a leader in the Alliance and prior working with the 7th Legion in Gilneas. He can be used as a bodyguard or support companion, which will cause him to appear and do an extremely large amount of damage in a wide AoE bladestorm.

### Battle for Azeroth[]

Crowley has returned leading a renewed Gilnean army into the Forsaken controlled territory of Hillsbrad following the [Battle for Lordaeron](/wiki/Battle_for_Lordaeron). He specifically marches against the [Sludge Fields](/wiki/Sludge_Fields), which are amassing undead to assail Gilneas.[11][12]

Crowley followed his king when they were tasked into assaulting [Orgrimmar](/wiki/Orgrimmar)'s [western gate](/wiki/Talon_Gate) while [Anduin Wrynn](/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) and [Varok Saurfang](/wiki/Varok_Saurfang)'s forces assaulted the [main gates](/wiki/Gates_of_Orgrimmar). Due to the [mak'gora](/wiki/Mak%27gora) between [Sylvanas Windrunner](/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner) and Saurfang, they did not assault the city.

## Notes and trivia[]

- Despite his appearance on the *Skyfire* following the Broken Shore, Crowley does not make an appearance at [Greywatch](/wiki/Greywatch), nor does he appear during the [Cove of Nashal](/wiki/Cove_of_Nashal) scenario. Numerous unused NPCs show that he may have been originally intended to accompany the player throughout [Stormheim](/wiki/Stormheim), rather than Lorna, or at the least have had more than one appearance, as it bears the [Gilneas Brigade](/wiki/Gilneas_Brigade) tag, which none of his prior appearances bear, including the *Skyfire*. He does, however, re-appear when a player achieves Prestige Rank 2 and is honored by the Alliance during [10-45] [A Royal Audience](/wiki/A_Royal_Audience_(Alliance)), where he stands beside Princess [Tess Greymane](/wiki/Tess_Greymane).
  - Darius was presumably originally slated to [appear](/wiki/Skyhold#Speculation) in [Skyhold](/wiki/Skyhold). Whether this would have been as a recently deceased spirit, or as a still living character (as numerous other still living [warriors](/wiki/Warrior) were also going to appear alongside ghosts of warriors past) is unknown, given his aforementioned appearance in Stormwind, it is unlikely he would have been slain. Darius and Eitrigg eventually did make it into Skyhold as followers in [7.2](/wiki/Patch_7.2.0).
- His [generic name](/wiki/Generic_name) in 7.2 is **Greywatch Mauler**.
- In *[Curse of the Worgen](/wiki/Curse_of_the_Worgen)*, it is discovered that Crowley could not resume his human form at the time of the invasion because he held onto guilt. It is unknown if this is still the case or if like the other worgen who undertook the worgen ritual, he was able to make peace with his emotions and bring balance to his human and worgen side.[5]
- There is a growing fanbase for Darius Crowley, mostly due to his ability to cleave mobs using only his fists during a quest in the worgen starting area. Due to this, it has been suggested by several players that he could finally be the [Alliance](/wiki/Alliance)'s version of [Varok Saurfang](/wiki/Varok_Saurfang).
- His name could be a reference to the 1980 song "[Mr Crowley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr_Crowley)" by [Ozzy Osbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozzy_Osbourne), which is in turn a reference to 20th-century occultist [Aleister Crowley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleister_Crowley). This song is also used in the meme growing around the character, with fans referring to the character as "Miiiiister Croooowwwwley" (dun dun dun!). Early in its existence, the Mr. Crowley meme was referenced by the voice actor Jesse Cox in his worgen starting zone videos.
- He could also be named after the Persian king [Darius the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darius_I).
- In the Gilneas starter zone, and outside of it in early beta builds, Crowley's worgen form wielded a two-handed sword, however he has since been changed to dual wield fist weapons. It's possible this was done intentionally by Blizzard in response to his fistcleaving meme.
- He is occasionally referred to as "Lord Box Head", "Darius Boxhead" or "Boxhead Crowley" due to his worgen model in early beta builds.
- Darius was originally voiced by [David Thewlis](/wiki/David_Thewlis), who portrays the werewolf [Remus Lupin](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Remus_Lupin) in the *Harry Potter* film franchise. In *[World of Warcraft: Legion](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion)* and *[Hearthstone](/wiki/Hearthstone_(game))* he is voiced by a new, currently unknown voice actor.
- One of Darius' irritated voicelines in *Legion* ("I'm about to lose control and I think I like it!") is a reference to the 1982 song "[I'm So Excited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I%27m_So_Excited)" by [The Pointer Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pointer_Sisters).
