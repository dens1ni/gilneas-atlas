# Lord Geoffery Tulvan

Lord Geoffery Tulvan is a worgen located at the Crucible of Carnage in the Twilight Highlands. He is one of the challengers and seeks to become the greatest warrior in the world. He is strong enough to lift his own carriage. Once he turns, he is known as Lord Geoffery Wildwolf.

He summons three servants.

## Objective of

- [30-35G5] The Crucible of Carnage: The Earl of Evisceration!

## Abilities

- Death by Peasant— Calls forth 3 servants of the House Barov[sic] that will fight, cook, and clean for you.
- Undying Frenzy— Chops, dices and minces your enemy.
- Undying Frenzy— Chops, dices and minces your enemy in 5 sec sec.
- Uppercut— Inflicts 110% weapon damage to an enemy, knocking it back.
