This article contains lore taken from Warcraft novels, novellas, or short stories.

Lord Marley was a Gilnean noble. King Genn Greymane had originally planned to have the Greymane Wall cut through Marley's domain, but later decided to have it cut through Lord Darius Crowley's domain instead at the recommendation of Lord Godfrey.[1] He participated in the secret worgen hunts with King Greymane, Lord Godfrey, Baron Ashbury, and a few other nobles in the Blackwald during full moons. 

His subsequent fate is unknown.
