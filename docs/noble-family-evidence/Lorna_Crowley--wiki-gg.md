**Lorna Crowley** is the daughter of Lord [Darius Crowley](/wiki/Darius_Crowley), who was one of the principal leaders during the [Northgate Rebellion](/wiki/Northgate_Rebellion). After the retaking of [Gilneas City](/wiki/Gilneas_City), she joined her father as commander of the [Gilneas Liberation Front](/wiki/Gilneas_Liberation_Front). When the [Burning Legion](/wiki/Burning_Legion) returned to assault Azeroth, Lorna and Darius joined [Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane) on the *[Skyfire](/wiki/Skyfire)* as part of his army forces.

## Background

### Fall of Gilneas

[![Comics title.png](/images/thumb/Comics_title.png/57px-Comics_title.png?427e0a)](/wiki/Comics) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [manga](/wiki/Manga) or [comics](/wiki/Comics).**

[![Cataclysm](/images/Cata-Logo-Small.png?cd16d8)](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm) **This section concerns content related to *[Cataclysm](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm)*.**

[![](/images/thumb/Lorna_Crowley.jpg/130px-Lorna_Crowley.jpg?7827a6)](/wiki/File:Lorna_Crowley.jpg)

[![](/images/thumb/Exodus_Carriage.jpg/320px-Exodus_Carriage.jpg?738c04)](/wiki/File:Exodus_Carriage.jpg)

The day of the [worgen](/wiki/Worgen) attack on Gilneas City, Lorna saved a worgen adventurer by shooting [Josiah Avery](/wiki/Josiah_Avery). The two of them worked together to get the [Northgate rebels](/wiki/Northgate_rebels)' hidden arsenal for Greymane's army.

Shortly after, she met with [King Genn](/wiki/Genn_Greymane) and [Prince Liam](/wiki/Liam_Greymane) after her father chose to stay behind in the [Light's Dawn Cathedral](/wiki/Light%27s_Dawn_Cathedral) and make a [last stand](/wiki/Last_Stand_(quest)) against the worgen. She had received word her father was free, but Liam told her about the choice Darius made. Lorna pulled out her gun and took aim at Genn, cursing at him and believing this was his idea of revenge after the [Northgate Rebellion](/wiki/Northgate_Rebellion). Liam restrained her, and a hysterical Lorna demanded she be let go to die with her father before she began to cry in Liam's arms. Afterward she returned to the [Crowley Orchard](/wiki/Crowley_Orchard) and lived as a hermit. Before being evacuated, she and the worgen adventurer worked together to rescue some horses. She then moved to the [Greymane Manor](/wiki/Greymane_Manor) and from there she took a carriage with [Gwen Armstead](/wiki/Gwen_Armstead) to [Stormglen](/wiki/Stormglen) where she found a diary of [Bradshaw](/wiki/Bradshaw) and learned that the man ventured into [Blackwald](/wiki/Blackwald). She sent him there only to find the night elf [Belysra Starbreeze](/wiki/Belysra_Starbreeze) along with other worgen. Darius was revealed to have had survived and become a worgen. He was worried how Lorna would respond to his curse, but she was fine with it.

She was then sent to [Livery Outpost](/wiki/Livery_Outpost) to rescue the villagers from northern Gilneas and free [Emberstone Village](/wiki/Emberstone_Village). Lorna then aided the [Worgen adventurers](/wiki/Worgen_(playable)) in retaking Gilneas City after the first assault of the Forsaken. After the death of Liam, she learned that the Forsaken were going to use the plague on the city. She was present at Liam's funeral in [Aderic's Repose](/wiki/Aderic%27s_Repose). After that she moved to [Keel Harbor](/wiki/Keel_Harbor) and loaded explosives onto an [Orc Gunship](/wiki/Gunship) and [used it as a ram](/wiki/Endgame_(quest)) to destroy the Forsaken forward camp next to the [Greymane Wall](/wiki/Greymane_Wall).

### Gilneas Liberation Front

Following the [invasion of Gilneas](/wiki/Invasion_of_Gilneas), Lorna joined her father as commander of the [Gilneas Liberation Front](/wiki/Gilneas_Liberation_Front) and fought with the [Alliance](/wiki/Alliance) to retake the [Ruins of Gilneas](/wiki/Ruins_of_Gilneas) from the Forsaken. She attacked the [Forsaken Forward Command](/wiki/Forsaken_Forward_Command) as she used ![](/images/thumb/Inv_gizmo_healthpotionpack.png/16px-Inv_gizmo_healthpotionpack.png?965787) [[Forsaken Communication Device]](/wiki/Forsaken_Communication_Device) to declare that the Alliance once again controlled Gilneas and their forces would soon arrive to kill those that would not surrender.[2] However, while stationed in [Silverpine Forest](/wiki/Silverpine_Forest) she was captured by the now-[Forsaken](/wiki/Forsaken) [Lord Godfrey](/wiki/Vincent_Godfrey). Lorna served as the bargaining chip in the surrender of the Gilneas Liberation Front as [Sylvanas](/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner) gave Darius two choices; surrender or have Lorna turned into a member of the Forsaken. Darius, unwilling to allow such a fate to befall his daughter, agreed to the surrender, and ordered the retreat towards the still contested territory of Gilneas.[3]

### Legion

[![Legion](/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440)](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion) **This section concerns content related to *[Legion](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion)*.**

[![](/images/thumb/Lorna_Crowley_Legion.jpg/160px-Lorna_Crowley_Legion.jpg?596cf5)](/wiki/File:Lorna_Crowley_Legion.jpg)

Following the [Battle for the Broken Shore](/wiki/Battle_for_the_Broken_Shore), Lorna and her father Darius joined [Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane) on the *[Skyfire](/wiki/Skyfire)*. Lorna and [Princess Tess](/wiki/Tess_Greymane) escorted the adventurer to the [Stormwind Keep](/wiki/Stormwind_Keep), conversing on the way. When flying to Stormwind Keep, Princess Tess Greymane confided to Lorna her concern about the lust for vengeance consuming her father. Lorna states that all Gilneans suffer from that rage, her father more than most. Though Tess concurs with Lorna, she fears more deaths are sure to come. Lorna grimly replies that such is the cost of vengeance and that Gilneas has waited for it long enough. When arriving at Stormwind Keep, Tess tells the adventurer to give King [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) their condolences for the death of his father [Varian Wrynn](/wiki/Varian_Wrynn). The two quickly return to the gunship once the player lands, as they are needed back at the ship.[4]

The [Gilneas Brigade](/wiki/Gilneas_Brigade) led by Lorna and her father Darius established [Greywatch](/wiki/Greywatch) to secure [Stormheim](/wiki/Stormheim) from threats.

During the Skyfire's assault on Sylvanas Windrunner's fleet, Lorna led the Alliance boarding party in scouring Windrunner's ships in their search for the Banshee Queen. After discovering that Sylvanas traveled to [Stormheim](/wiki/Stormheim) ahead of the fleet and that the Horde was planning on infiltrating the Skyfire, Lorna and her soldiers went back to the Skyfire to defend the ship. Unfortunately, the Horde succeded in setting fire to the Skyfire's ammunition and its explosions caused the Skyfire to crash and burn on Stormheim. Though the crew of the Skyfire was able to evacuate before the crash, the gunship was utterly destroyed.

Lorna regrouped with her Gilneas Brigade compatriots and acted as leader of Greymane's scouts in [Lorna's Watch](/wiki/Lorna%27s_Watch). Lorna and her Gilneas Brigade scouts would aid Greymane's mercenaries in undergoing the various ancient trials necessary to obtain the [Aegis of Aggramar](/wiki/Aegis_of_Aggramar). Following a lead on Sylvanas, Lorna would scout [Skold-Ashil](/wiki/Skold-Ashil) on Greymane's orders to uncover what Sylvanas seeks there. Lorna discovers that Sylvanas means to undergo the rituals of ascension to be granted an audience with [Eyir](/wiki/Eyir) so that she could subjugate her and create more [Val'kyr](/wiki/Val%27kyr). Lorna sends news of her findings to Genn so that he could confront Sylvanas personally and undermine her plot. Greymane succeeded in freeing Eyir and Lorna recovers a poisoned Genn and treats him back at Greywatch.

### Battle for Azeroth

[![Battle for Azeroth](/images/thumb/BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png/40px-BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png?25e36c)](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth) **This section concerns content related to *[Battle for Azeroth](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth)*.**

| [![](/images/thumb/Stub.png/17px-Stub.png?fdeef6)](/wiki/File:Stub.png) | This section is **a [lore](/wiki/Lore) stub**. |
|---|---|
Lorna was present at the war council in [Stormwind Keep](/wiki/Stormwind_Keep).[5] She later participated in the [Battle for Darkshore](/wiki/Battle_for_Darkshore).

Lorna followed her king when they were tasked into assaulting [Orgrimmar](/wiki/Orgrimmar)'s [western gate](/wiki/Talon_Gate) while [Anduin Wrynn](/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) and [Varok Saurfang](/wiki/Varok_Saurfang)'s forces assaulted the [main gates](/wiki/Gates_of_Orgrimmar). Due to the [mak'gora](/wiki/Mak%27gora) between [Sylvanas Windrunner](/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner) and Saurfang, they did not assault the city.

## Lock the Doors

[![](/images/thumb/CotW_Lorna_Crowley.jpg/160px-CotW_Lorna_Crowley.jpg?43edb8)](/wiki/File:CotW_Lorna_Crowley.jpg)

Gilneas

- Dear [Margaret](/wiki/Margaret),
I heeded your advice from last week to board up my windows and buy a reinforced door. None too soon, either: the [Starlight Slasher](/wiki/Starlight_Slasher) struck a household not two blocks from my own last night. I inquired with the authorities at the scene about what they're doing to reign in the murder spree, but they didn't even grant me a second glance, waving me off with the usual "Stay off the streets and lock your door!" I know most of them don't trust me, but they're not proving to be trustworthy themselves, either.

Well, I'm not one to sit around, waiting to get dragged off in the middle of the night! I've started training the [mastiff](/wiki/Mastiff) pups I bought from [Rupert](/wiki/Rupert) on a lark last month to track a scent, and they've taken to it quite well. They'll make fine hunting and guard dogs soon, so I'll be happy to pass on off to you once they're able to track a scent decently: they're right pleasant company, it'll help your young ones sleep better. That's at least a month off, though, so just consider the offer for now.

Which brings me to the reason for this letter: the attached package is a blunderbuss, so be sure to unwrap it at home and keep it somewhere hidden, but accessible. I threw some spare powder and buckshot in there too - no, no need to thank me. You helped [my father](/wiki/Darius_Crowley) when he was in a rough spot, and [Crowleys](/wiki/Crowley) never forget kindness freely given. Take the gun and keep safe. I fear things are going to get worse before they get better.

Sincerely,

 Lorna Crowley[7]

## Notes and trivia

- She can be seen using a rifle or a sword / shield combination.
  - Lorna's actual class seems to shift depending on her appearances, being seen using warrior abilities and hunter abilities. In one strange instance, she also uses a [rogue](/wiki/Rogue) ability in the form of Backstab.
- Despite being from Gilneas and involved with the Gilneas Liberation Front, Lorna shows no signs of being afflicted by the [worgen curse](/wiki/Worgen_curse). Indeed, during their confrontation in ![H](/images/Horde_15.png?c852a8) [5-30] [Cities in Dust](/wiki/Cities_in_Dust), Sylvanas specifically points this out to Darius, asking him why he did not offer his blood to her to make her a worgen (and thus immune to Val'kyr resurrection) if he truly cared for her. It is likely because he did not want his daughter to be afflicted with the Curse of the Worgen, as he is.
- According to [Astrid Langstrump](/wiki/Astrid_Langstrump) when spoken to without being exalted with Gilneas, Lorna continues to breed and supply her people with ![](/images/thumb/Ability_mount_ridinghorse.png/16px-Ability_mount_ridinghorse.png?ddceb2) [[Mountain Horse]](/wiki/Mountain_Horse) steeds, and would be upset should Astrid to sell it to anyone not exalted with their people. Given Lorna's current location in Gilneas, it would explain why Astrid is taking care of distribution of the horses in her stead.
- Lorna tends to have a rose in her hair when in civilian clothing.
- During the *[World of Warcraft: Cataclysm](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm)* beta she was watched over by a [Son of Goldrinn](/wiki/Son_of_Goldrinn) while staying at the [Crowley Orchard](/wiki/Crowley_Orchard).
- Lorna is voiced by [Gillian Wiggin](/wiki/Gillian_Wiggin).
