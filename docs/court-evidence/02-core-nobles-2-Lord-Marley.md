**Lord Marley** was a [Gilnean](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)) noble. King [Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane) had originally planned to have the [Greymane Wall](/wiki/Greymane_Wall) cut through Marley's domain, but later decided to have it cut through [Lord Darius Crowley](/wiki/Lord_Darius_Crowley)'s domain instead at the recommendation of [Lord Godfrey](/wiki/Lord_Godfrey).[1] He participated in the secret worgen hunts with King Greymane, Lord Godfrey, [Baron Ashbury](/wiki/Baron_Ashbury), and a few other nobles in the [Blackwald](/wiki/Blackwald) during full moons.

His subsequent fate is unknown.
