**Lord Mayor Morrison** is a [human](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Human) and leader of the [Pyrewood Council](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Pyrewood_Council) who appears in [Pyrewood Village](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Pyrewood_Village) in [Silverpine Forest](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Silverpine_Forest_(Classic)) during ![H](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/c/c4/Horde_15.png/revision/latest?cb=20201010153315) [15] [Pyrewood Ambush](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Pyrewood_Ambush).
