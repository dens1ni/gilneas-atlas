**Shadowfang Keep** is a haunted [keep](/wiki/Keep) not far from the borders of [Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_peninsula) in southern [Silverpine Forest](/wiki/Silverpine_Forest), on a high bluff overlooking [Pyrewood Village](/wiki/Pyrewood_Village). It was named for the forces of its former master,[2] the demented [Archmage Arugal](/wiki/Archmage_Arugal), who wrested the keep from [Baron Silverlaine](/wiki/Baron_Silverlaine) with his [worgen](/wiki/Worgen) "[children](/wiki/Son_of_Arugal)" and held it until he was defeated by a group of [Horde](/wiki/Horde) heroes.[1][3] The keep was then occupied by the Scourge remnants and followers of the renegade [Gilnean](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)) nobleman [Vincent Godfrey](/wiki/Vincent_Godfrey), traitor to both his homeland and to the [Forsaken](/wiki/Forsaken) who returned him from death.

During the [Fourth War](/wiki/Fourth_War), the keep was taken by the [Forsaken](/wiki/Forsaken), who captured it and were using it as a bio-weapons facility to wage war against Gilneas. By the end of the war, the [Bloodfang pack](/wiki/Bloodfang_pack) had taken control of the keep, moving most of its artifacts to [Fenris Keep](/wiki/Fenris_Keep) to be guarded closely.[1]

##
History

Shadowfang Keep was previously a part of the [kingdom of Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)). It was the home to the lords of [Pyrewood Village](/wiki/Pyrewood_Village), who answered to [Darius Crowley](/wiki/Darius_Crowley)'s family.[4] The lord of the castle was [Baron Silverlaine](/wiki/Baron_Silverlaine) and his forces were led by [Commander Springvale](/wiki/Commander_Springvale), a [paladin](/wiki/Paladin) of the [Silver Hand](/wiki/Silver_Hand). [Jordan Stilwell](/wiki/Jordan_Stilwell) and [Daphne Stilwell](/wiki/Daphne_Stilwell) used to live and work here before the [Third War](/wiki/Third_War). [Landen Stilwell](/wiki/Landen_Stilwell) was still here years later.

When the [Scourge](/wiki/Scourge) washed over Lordaeron, the Gilnean Archmage, [Arugal](/wiki/Arugal), investigated the research of the mage [Ur](/wiki/Ur). With the sorcerer's knowledge, Arugal summoned the [worgen](/wiki/Worgen) with the blessings of [Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane)[5] and against the advice of the [Kirin Tor](/wiki/Kirin_Tor).[6] The worgen successfully turned the tide against the Scourge, but became uncontrollable, attacking the [Gilnean military](/wiki/Gilnean_military). After this, Arugal's worgen ran rampant through Silverpine, eventually overwhelming the keep, killing Baron Silverlaine and his family. Distraught and broken, Arugal accepted the worgen as his [children](/wiki/Son_of_Arugal) and retreated into the keep and named the place Shadowfang Keep.[1] Communing with [Alpha Prime](/wiki/Alpha_Prime), he became a key servant of the [Wolf Cult](/wiki/Wolf_Cult), converting the humans of Silverpine into worgen, namely the [Moonrage](/wiki/Moonrage) and [Shadowfang packs](/wiki/Shadowfang_pack). Protected by his worgen minions as well as the ghosts of the fallen Baron and his household, Arugal served his master in converting most of the forest's remaining denizens to the curse, with locations such as [Deep Elem Mine](/wiki/Deep_Elem_Mine) falling as well as Pyrewood.[7]

After sometime, the entire [Pyrewood Village](/wiki/Pyrewood_Village) was turned to the [worgen curse](/wiki/Worgen_curse), turning into the beasts at night. During the day, they were human and willing to provide supplies to [Alliance](/wiki/Alliance) travelers, though the keep was never friendly to these adventurers. They were led by the [Pyrewood Council](/wiki/Pyrewood_Council), who were all servants of Arugal. The Forsaken became increasingly suspicious of their activities, especially after the disappearance of one of their apothecaries, [Apothecary Berard](/wiki/Apothecary_Berard). After it was discovered that Arugal had transformed him into a worgen, the Forsaken assassinated the council.[8]

Arugal was eventually defeated and beheaded by agents of the [Horde](/wiki/Horde).[1][9] Following this, the only denizens of the keep in the years to follow were the [Crown Chemical Co.](/wiki/Crown_Chemical_Co.), who used the location as a base of operations for their schemes.

### Wrath of the Lich King

The Lich King saw it fit to bring Arugal back as one of his puppets,[1] so several [San'layn](/wiki/San%27layn) blood princes arrived at Shadowfang Keep and found Arugal's grave, raising the Archmage as a shade to serve the [Scourge](/wiki/Scourge).[10]

Continuing his work converting humans to a wolf cult, the now undead archmage makes his home in a tower built and named for the keep, [Shadowfang Tower](/wiki/Shadowfang_Tower). Here, he and his new wolf cult were defeated as well.

### Cataclysm

**This section concerns content related to *[Cataclysm](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm)*.**

After betraying both Gilneas and the Forsaken, [Vincent Godfrey](/wiki/Vincent_Godfrey), [Baron Ashbury](/wiki/Baron_Ashbury), and [Lord Walden](/wiki/Lord_Walden) converted the abandoned keep into their holdfast against their enemies. The keep became a battleground between the [Alliance](/wiki/Alliance) and [Horde](/wiki/Horde) vying for Godfrey's head, with both groups aiming to slaughter the man as well as claim the keep for their respective factions. These operations were led by [Ivar Bloodfang](/wiki/Ivar_Bloodfang) and [Deathstalker Commander Belmont](/wiki/Deathstalker_Commander_Belmont) respectively.

### Battle for Azeroth

The [Forsaken](/wiki/Forsaken) gained control over Shadowfang Keep, turning it into a bio-weapons facility where they began to produce plague to unleash against Gilneas. Meanwhile, the [7th Legion](/wiki/7th_Legion) troops march towards Shadowfang in order to prevent its deployment.[11]

### Exploring Azeroth

When [Mathias Shaw](/wiki/Mathias_Shaw) and [Flynn Fairwind](/wiki/Flynn_Fairwind) embarked on their journey to explore Azeroth as well as catalogue the locations of various artifacts, they visited Silverpine forest.

Shaw noted his main contact in the area as Ivar Bloodfang, stating that the packleader was his contact within Shadowfang Keep. The pack has apparently claimed it, alongside Fenris Isle, and has taken most of the artifacts within the Shadowfang to Fenris Keep to be guarded closely.[1]

### Dragonflight

While Shadowfang Keep is never visited during it; the [Forsaken](/wiki/Undead_(playable)) [heritage armor](/wiki/Heritage_armor) questline establishes that, somehow, the [Scarlet Crusade](/wiki/Scarlet_Crusade) has taken command of [Fenris Keep](/wiki/Fenris_Keep). By the end of said questline, the keep is taken over by the Forsaken.[12] This leaves Shadowfang as the last known major holding of the Bloodfang pack in Silverpine.

##
Dungeon Journals

### WoW Dungeon Journal

*During the [Third War](/wiki/Third_War), the wizards of the [Kirin Tor](/wiki/Kirin_Tor) battled against the undead armies of the [Scourge](/wiki/Scourge). When the wizards of [Dalaran](/wiki/Dalaran) died in battle, they would rise soon after - adding their former might to the growing Scourge. Frustrated by their lack of progress (and against the advice of his peers) the [Archmage](/wiki/Archmage), [Arugal](/wiki/Arugal) elected to summon extra-dimensional entities to bolster Dalaran's diminishing ranks. Arugal's summoning brought the ravenous worgen into the world of [Azeroth](/wiki/Azeroth). The feral wolf-men slaughtered not only the Scourge, but quickly turned on the wizards themselves. The worgen sieged the keep of the noble, [Baron Silverlaine](/wiki/Baron_Silverlaine). Situated above the tiny hamlet of [Pyrewood](/wiki/Pyrewood), the keep quickly fell into shadow and ruin. Driven mad with guilt, Arugal adopted the worgen as his children and retreated to the newly dubbed 'Shadowfang Keep'. It's said he still resides there, protected by his massive pet, Fenrus - and haunted by the vengeful ghost of Baron Silverlaine.*[13]

### Cataclysm Dungeon Journal

**This section concerns content related to *[Cataclysm](/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm)*.**

*Looming over Pyrewood Village from the southern bluffs of Silverpine Forest, Shadowfang Keep casts a shadow as dark as its legacy. Sinister forces occupy these ruins, formerly the dwelling of the mad archmage Arugal's worgen. The restless shade of Baron Silverlaine lingers while Lord Godfrey and his cabal of erstwhile Gilnean noblemen plot against their enemies both living and undead.*

##
Overview

The quests and storyline for Alliance players and Horde players are different. The quest giver for Alliance players is [Ivar Bloodfang](/wiki/Ivar_Bloodfang), and the Horde quest giver is [Deathstalker Commander Belmont](/wiki/Deathstalker_Commander_Belmont). However, the quests for both factions are similar in that both are about the retaking of Shadowfang Keep from [Vincent Godfrey](/wiki/Vincent_Godfrey). The quests for each faction ( [8-30D] [Fury of the Pack](/wiki/Fury_of_the_Pack) and [8-30D] [Sweet, Merciless Revenge](/wiki/Sweet,_Merciless_Revenge)) end with their respective faction taking Shadowfang Keep.

Love is in the Air

**The subject of this article or section is part of [Love is in the Air](/wiki/Love_is_in_the_Air)**, a [seasonal event](/wiki/Event#Seasonal_Events) that lasts two weeks. Once the event has run its course, this will no longer be available until next year, but there are no guarantees.

Part of the [Love is in the Air](/wiki/Love_is_in_the_Air) event takes place in Shadowfang Keep. [Adventurers](/wiki/Adventurer) are sent by the [Steamwheedle Cartel](/wiki/Steamwheedle_Cartel) to investigate [Crown Chemical Company](/wiki/Crown_Chemical_Company) inside Shadowfang Keep. Defeating the Apothecary Trio awards the achievement [[Tough Love]](/wiki/Tough_Love).

##
In the RPG

The [Worgen Curse](/wiki/Worgen_Curse) afflicts the inhabitants of this dark [castle](/wiki/Castle). Unlike [Pyrewood](/wiki/Pyrewood)'s good folk, Shadowfang's lords were bastards to begin with. They revel in their curse and eagerly lope into the moonlight to spill blood and rip warm flesh from living bones. Their howls can be heard as far away as [Ambermill](/wiki/Ambermill).[14]
