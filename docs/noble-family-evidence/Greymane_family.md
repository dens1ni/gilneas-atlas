# Greymane family

- *Were you looking for the [Greymane Wall](/wiki/Greymane_Wall)?*
The **Greymane** family (also known as the **Greymane clan**)[1] is the ruling line of the [kingdom of Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)). The family ruled from [Greymane Manor](/wiki/Greymane_Manor) and had a Royal Chemist in the form of [Krennan Aranas](/wiki/Krennan_Aranas).

## Known members[]

- [King Archibald Greymane](/wiki/Archibald_Greymane) - father of Genn and former king of Gilneas.
  - [King Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane) (and [Queen Mia Greymane](/wiki/Mia_Greymane)) - Ruler of the human and worgen of Gilneas, affected by the [worgen](/wiki/Worgen) curse himself. Mia is the wife of Genn and mother of Tess and Liam.[2]
    - [Prince Liam Greymane](/wiki/Liam_Greymane) - son of Genn Greymane. Died during the [Battle for Gilneas City](/wiki/The_Battle_for_Gilneas_City).
    - [Princess Tess Greymane](/wiki/Tess_Greymane), daughter of Genn Greymane.[3]

## Trivia[]

- *[Tides of Darkness](/wiki/Tides_of_Darkness)* spells the name as "Graymane".
- There are also an unrelated [Crispin Greymane](/wiki/Crispin_Greymane) dwarf and an [Elder Graymane](/wiki/Elder_Graymane) taunka.
