Lord Candren is the quartermaster for Gilneas. He is found in Darnassus's Temple Gardens, near the flight master.

 This section concerns content related to Battle for Azeroth.

Lord Candren survived the Burning of Teldrassil and made his way to Stormwind Embassy.
