**Isiden Perenolde**[1] was the nephew of Lord [Aiden Perenolde](/wiki/Aiden_Perenolde), King of [Alterac](/wiki/Alterac_(kingdom)). When Alterac was placed under martial law by order of King [Terenas](/wiki/Terenas_Menethil_II), Isiden fled to [Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_peninsula), where he hoped to rally support.[2] He was eventually backed by [Genn Greymane](/wiki/Genn_Greymane), [Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom))' king, as the matter of the succession began to heat up.[3]

Nothing has been heard from Isiden since then and his status is unknown.
