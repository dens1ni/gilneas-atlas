Baron Longshore is the former captain of the Heedless, a ship in the pirate fleet of the Southsea Freebooters, and hails from Gilneas.[1] He is located on the Merchant Coast in the Northern Barrens.

Background[]

Gazlowe put a prize on Baron Longshore's head on charges of piracy and for leading vessels of the Southsea Freebooters.[1]

Cataclysm[]

 This section concerns content related to Cataclysm.

After the Cataclysm, his fleet was decimated by the Alliance navy. He and the surviving Southsea Cutthroats can be found incarcerated by the Northwatch Expeditionary Unit.[2] Horde adventurers are able to rescue him in a quest, in exchange for the location of his stolen treasure. He eventually double-crosses the adventurers and attacks them.
