**Lord Hiram Creed** was a [black](/wiki/Black_dragonflight)[drakonid](/wiki/Drakonid) who disguises themselves as an affluent [Gilnean](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)) noble. Creed manipulated a group of Gilnean humans and worgen named the [Blackhowl](/wiki/Blackhowl) who occupied [Gilneas City](/wiki/Gilneas_City), and secretly infected them with his own draconic blood, which made them stronger but also unwittingly slowly turned them into his unwilling servants. The Blackhowl had apparently repelled numerous [Forsaken](/wiki/Forsaken) assaults, as seen in the courtyard of the [Stoneward Prison](/wiki/Stoneward_Prison) where Creed resided, which was littered with Forsaken corpses.

Black Prince [Wrathion](/wiki/Wrathion) sent his [rogue](/wiki/Rogue)[champion](/wiki/Adventurer) who sought to earn his favor to assassinate Creed in his crusade to eliminate all corrupted black dragons on [Azeroth](/wiki/Azeroth).[1] With his death, it is unknown what the fate of the Blackhowl group is.
