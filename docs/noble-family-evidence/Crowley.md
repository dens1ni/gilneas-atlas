**Crowley** is the name of a notorious noble family of [Gilneas](/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)). They owned the lands of [Pyrewood Village](/wiki/Pyrewood_Village) and [Ambermill](/wiki/Ambermill). They also were the owners of [Crowley Orchard](/wiki/Crowley_Orchard) and [Crowley Stable Grounds](/wiki/Crowley_Stable_Grounds).
