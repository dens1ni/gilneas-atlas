**Lord Geoffery Tulvan** is a [worgen](/wiki/Worgen) located at the [Crucible of Carnage](/wiki/Crucible_of_Carnage) in the [Twilight Highlands](/wiki/Twilight_Highlands). He is one of the challengers and seeks to become the greatest warrior in the world. He is strong enough to lift his own carriage. Once he turns, he is known as **Lord Geoffery Wildwolf**.

He summons three [servants](/wiki/Servant_of_Lord_Geoffery).
