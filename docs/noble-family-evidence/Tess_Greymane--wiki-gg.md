**Tess Greymane** is the Queen of [Gilneas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)), and a member of the [Greymane clan](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Greymane_family) as the daughter of [King Genn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Genn_Greymane) and [Queen Mia](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mia_Greymane).[2] Once the princess of the kingdom, Tess became the rightful heir to the throne after her brother, [Prince Liam](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Liam_Greymane), was murdered by [Sylvanas Windrunner](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner) during the [Battle for Gilneas City](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Battle_for_Gilneas_City). In the course of the [invasion of Gilneas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Invasion_of_Gilneas), she and her people were exiled to the [Howling Oak](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Howling_Oak) in [Darnassus](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Darnassus) with the help of the [night elves](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Night_elf), until she moved with the royal family to [Stormwind City](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stormwind_City). With her brother gone, she was searching for answers to questions she never thought she would ask.[3]

By the time of the [third invasion of the Burning Legion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion), Tess fought the [demons](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Demon) as a member of the [Uncrowned](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Uncrowned) and joined the [Council of Shadows](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Council_of_Shadows),[4] the leaders of the secret [rogue](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Rogue) organization. During the [Fourth War](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fourth_War), she and [Lorna Crowley](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lorna_Crowley) headed to [Darkshore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkshore) on behalf of King Genn, where they led the Gilnean forces to help the [Army of the Black Moon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Army_of_the_Black_Moon) in reclaiming their land during the [Battle for Darkshore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Battle_for_Darkshore).[5]

Over a decade after their exile, King Genn declared that the time had come to reclaim their kingdom and gave command of the operation to Tess.[6] At the head of the Gilnean troops and their allies of the [7th Legion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/7th_Legion), she also appealed for support from the [Desolate Council](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Desolate_Council), the [Forsaken](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken) being experts in fighting the [Scarlet Crusade](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Scarlet_Crusade). Following the [Reclamation of Gilneas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Reclamation_of_Gilneas) and its capital, King Genn reaffirmed his faith in Tess and abdicated as the ruling king of Gilneas, declaring that his daughter would henceforth reign as the new queen of Gilneas, ready to lead their people towards a better future.[7]

## History

### Childhood

Tess was born to [Queen Mia](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mia_Greymane) and [King Genn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Genn_Greymane) before [Gilneas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)) left the [Alliance of Lordaeron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alliance_of_Lordaeron) after the [Second War](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Second_War).[8] Her life was later saved by a potion made by the Royal Chemist [Krennan Aranas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Krennan_Aranas); if not for him, she would have died soon after she was born.[2] When she was a little girl, her father told her that she was to find one beautiful thing every day and that it would make the hard days easier.[9] Genn was also gruff with his children and rarely told them he loved them.[10]

### Curse of the Worgen

[![Comics title.png](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Comics_title.png/57px-Comics_title.png?427e0a)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Comics) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [manga](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Manga) or [comics](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Comics).**

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/CotW_Tess_Greymane.jpg/240px-CotW_Tess_Greymane.jpg?21373a)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:CotW_Tess_Greymane.jpg)

After the [Cataclysm](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Cataclysm_(event)) destroyed the town of [Duskhaven](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Duskhaven), Tess ventured up to her father's observatory in [Greymane Manor](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Greymane_Manor) with a single [peacebloom](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Peacebloom) flower. She reminded Genn of the words he would tell her as a little girl, and that even among the ruined Gilneas she was able to find an unharmed peacebloom, which gave them both hope.[9]

### Cataclysm

[![Cataclysm](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/Cata-Logo-Small.png?cd16d8)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm) **This section concerns content related to *[Cataclysm](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm)*.**

Due to [Prince Liam Greymane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Liam_Greymane)'s death, Tess became the heir to the Gilnean throne.[11] With her brother gone, she was searching for answers to questions she never thought she would ask.[3]

### Lord of His Pack

[![WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [novels](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels), [novellas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novellas), or [short stories](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Short_stories).**

During the exodus to [Darnassus](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Darnassus) in 28 [ADP](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/ADP), Tess and her mother, [Mia Greymane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mia_Greymane), were aboard the *[Elune's Radiance](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Elune%27s_Radiance)* while her father was on another ship. The fleet was hit by a powerful storm generated by the [Cataclysm](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Cataclysm_(event)), and the ship started to sink. Having a leg broken during the storm, Mia could not move, and Tess remained with her, refusing to leave, so they both risked drowning. Genn, refusing to lose the last two members of his family, assumed the [worgen](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Worgen) form and joined the rescue efforts of the [night elf](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Night_elf) sailors from his ship. After the storm, both women reached [Teldrassil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Teldrassil) safely.[12]

### Legion

[![Legion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion) **This section concerns content related to *[Legion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion)*.**

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Darius_and_Tess_ceremony.png/320px-Darius_and_Tess_ceremony.png?65bc7e)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Darius_and_Tess_ceremony.png)

Following the [Battle for the Broken Shore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Battle_for_the_Broken_Shore) in 32 ADP, Tess appeared on the *[Skyfire](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Skyfire)* [gunship](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gunship) that brought the [adventurers](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventurer) and other wounded back to [Stormwind City](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stormwind_City). If the adventurer chose to ride a [gryphon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gryphon) to [Stormwind Keep](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stormwind_Keep), she and [Lorna Crowley](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lorna_Crowley) would escort them on their own gryphons. While flying on the way, Tess confided to Lorna her concern about the lust for vengeance consuming her father, and Lorna stated that all Gilneans suffer from that rage, her [father](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Darius_Crowley) more than most. Though Tess concurred with her, she feared more deaths were sure to come, and Lorna grimly replied that such was the cost of vengeance and that Gilneas had waited for it long enough. When arriving at Stormwind Keep, Tess told the adventurer to give [High King Anduin Wrynn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) their condolences for the death of [King Varian](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Varian_Wrynn). The two then quickly returned to the gunship as they were needed.[13] While Tess was then dressed in armor, it is unknown if she actually had a military role, or if it was due to formal reasons.

Afterward, Tess was seen as one of the Shadows of the [Uncrowned](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Uncrowned), within the sewers of [Dalaran](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dalaran) in the secret [Hall of Shadows](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hall_of_Shadows). She sent the newest Shadow of the Uncrowned after [Garona Halforcen](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Garona_Halforcen), who had gone to Stormwind City to assassinate members of a [Burning Legion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Burning_Legion) cult and obtain the [The Kingslayers](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Kingslayers).[14]

She was also present alongside Lord Darius Crowley, and without her armor, when King Anduin commended the Alliance adventurer during a prestige ceremony for their battles against the Horde.[15]

When the Uncrowned received word of a large cave at the [Broken Shore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Broken_Shore), where the Legion was plotting against the [Armies of Legionfall](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Armies_of_Legionfall), Tess and [Fleet Admiral Tethys](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fleet_Admiral_Tethys) formulated a plan to stop them. With [fel](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fel)-infused gun powder taken from the [Blacksail Crew](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Blacksail_Crew),[16] Tess and the [Shadowblade](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventurer) sneaked their way into the [Lost Temple](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lost_Temple) and blew the cave up.[17] Afterwards, Tess explained that she had not yet pledged service to the Shadowblade because, while all rogues stepped into the shadows, it had been especially important that her status remained truly secret. But with the Legion's threat too great, Tess wanted to have a greater role in taking them down.[18]

### Before the Storm

[![WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [novels](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels), [novellas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novellas), or [short stories](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Short_stories).**

After [High King Anduin Wrynn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) returned from his trip to [Ironforge](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ironforge) and [Netherlight Temple](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Netherlight_Temple), he had dinner with [Genn Greymane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Genn_Greymane) in [Stormwind Keep](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stormwind_Keep). During the conversation, Genn urged Anduin to search for a queen to bear him a successor and suggested his own daughter, since Tess was close to Anduin's age and not some complete stranger. However, Anduin found the idea of an arranged marriage abhorrent and noted that Tess was a strong-willed woman with her own life, and who would likely not be particularly interested in the prospect of an arranged marriage either. Anduin internally reflected that he had heard all kinds of rumors about Tess' [actions](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Uncrowned), suggesting that she had "taken a [page or two](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Rogue)" from [Mathias Shaw](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Master_Mathias_Shaw), but he was unwilling to bring them up to Greymane, let alone after just having her be suggested as a bride.[19]

### Elegy

[![WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [novels](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels), [novellas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novellas), or [short stories](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Short_stories).**

During the [War of the Thorns](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/War_of_the_Thorns) in 33 ADP, when discussing who would head to [Darnassus](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Darnassus) to represent the kingdom of Gilneas, [High King Anduin Wrynn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) suggested that Tess should go and aid their people. While her father [King Genn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Genn_Greymane) agreed, he then stated that he did not know where she was at the time; instead leading to [Queen Mia](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mia_Greymane) being sent instead.[20]

### Battle for Azeroth

[![Battle for Azeroth](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png/40px-BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png?25e36c)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth) **This section concerns content related to *[Battle for Azeroth](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth)*.**

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Tess_Greymane_worgen.jpg/220px-Tess_Greymane_worgen.jpg?e5565f)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Tess_Greymane_worgen.jpg)

During the [Fourth War](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fourth_War), Tess, alongside [Lorna Crowley](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lorna_Crowley), headed to Darkshore on behalf of her father. There, surrounded by [Gilnean Royal Guards](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gilnean_Royal_Guard), she aided the [night elves](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Night_elf) of the [Army of the Black Moon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Army_of_the_Black_Moon) in reclaiming their land, acting as a co-commander with Lorna as they led the Gilnean forces in the region. She later stated she would not just stand by while the kaldorei needed their help, citing how her mother nearly lost her life; as well as claiming that [some atrocities](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/War_of_the_Thorns) cannot go unanswered.

During the [battle at the Gates of Orgrimmar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Battle_at_the_Gates_of_Orgrimmar), Tess followed her father when his forces were tasked by [High King Anduin Wrynn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn) to flank the city alongside the western gate. After they successfully captured the Orgrimmar's entrance from the [Northern Barrens](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Northern_Barrens), she could be found sneaking on the roof of the bridge.[21]

After the end of the war in 34 ADP, Tess wished to become a [worgen](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Worgen) so she could better know the plight of her people to better rule them when she becomes queen. While her parents were against it, they asked the night elf druid [Vassandra Stormclaw](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Vassandra_Stormclaw) to help Tess understand the worgen's plight without becoming a worgen herself. In [Duskwood](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Duskwood), Vassandra called on [Goldrinn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Goldrinn) at the [Twilight Glade](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Twilight_Glade) and the [Wild God](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Wild_God) put Tess into a dream scenario within the [Emerald Dream](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Emerald_Dream). In her dream, Tess was a worgen at the time of the [Forsaken](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken) [Invasion of Gilneas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Invasion_of_Gilneas), where she and the [Gilnean champion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventurer) relived the [battle for Gilneas City](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Battle_for_Gilneas_City). While she managed to kill the apparitions of [Nathanos Blightcaller](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nathanos_Blightcaller) and [Dark Ranger Thyala](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dark_Ranger_Thyala), she was unable to save her brother from his death and fell into the rage of the curse. After the champion defeated the [Essence of Rage](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Essence_of_Rage) that she manifested, and with Goldrinn's help, Tess realized that she did not need to be a worgen to understand her people, for it was being Gilnean in spite of the curse that defined them. She later promoted the Gilnean adventurer who aided her in this journey to a member of the [Greyguard](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Greyguard), in thanks and as recognition of their contribution to Gilneas.[22]

### Shadowlands

[![Shadowlands](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Shadowlands-Icon-Inline.png/48px-Shadowlands-Icon-Inline.png?d84a63)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Shadowlands) **This section concerns content related to *[Shadowlands](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Shadowlands)*.**

Following the armistice of the [Fourth War](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fourth_War), Tess was present in [Bashal'Aran](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Bashal%27Aran) and witnessed [Tyrande Whisperwind](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tyrande_Whisperwind)'s speech about the [Horde](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Horde)'s defeat in [Darkshore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkshore).[23]

### Dragonflight

[![Dragonflight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dragonflight-Icon-Inline.png/48px-Dragonflight-Icon-Inline.png?be4c0e)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight) **This section concerns content related to *[Dragonflight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight)*.**

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Tess_gates_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg/320px-Tess_gates_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg?c1afcf)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Tess_gates_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg)

During the [war against the Primalists](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/War_against_the_Primalists) in 40 ADP, Tess was amongst those who came to [Alexstrasza](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alexstrasza)'s aid when she was attacked by [Fyrakk the Blazing](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fyrakk). In the battle that followed, she fought alongside [Cenarius](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Cenarius), [Shandris Feathermoon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shandris_Feathermoon), [Maiev](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Maiev), and [Jarod Shadowsong](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jarod_Shadowsong) against Fyrakk's forces at the [Field of Flames](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Field_of_Flames).[24] After the death of Fyrakk, she attended the gathering at [Amirdrassil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Amirdrassil) when it bloomed on the [Dragon Isles](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dragon_Isles) and could be found alongside some faction leaders, including [Thrall](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thrall), [Baine Bloodhoof](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Baine_Bloodhoof), [Mayla Highmountain](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mayla_Highmountain), [Tyrande Whisperwind](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tyrande_Whisperwind), [Lilian Voss](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lilian_Voss) and [Jaina Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jaina_Proudmoore).[25]

As per their agreement with [Calia Menethil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Calia_Menethil), the [Forsaken](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken) had pulled out of Gilneas. However, in their absence, the [Scarlet Crusade](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Scarlet_Crusade) moved into the area. Knowing precious little of the Scarlet Crusade, Tess made the choice to turn to the [Forsaken](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Forsaken) for aid. In response, Lilian and Calia answered, and the trio devised a plan in which the Forsaken army would serve as a distraction for the Crusade, while a small strike team sneaked into the city and opened the gates for the Gilneas army. Though initially outraged at the Forsaken presence, [Genn Greymane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Genn_Greymane) gave command of the operation to Tess, which allowed for the plan to move forward.[26][8] While Tess and Voss joined the strike team, Calia gathered the Forsaken forces.[27]

Knowing that Voss served the Crusade in life, Tess asked if she was truly all right with clashing with her former comrades. In response, Voss thanked her for asking and admitted that the Crusade meant nothing to her and that at the moment of her death, she meant nothing to them.[28] While clashing against the Crusade, Tess had the team gather the [explosives](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Scarlet_Explosives) the Scarlets had gathered in order to turn the Crusade's own weapon against as a means to need to clear a way for their forces to advance.[29] After clashing against the Crusaders within [Gilneas City](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gilneas_City), the strike team was successful in opening the gates for the Gilnean army. As the Alliance forces surged forward, Tess used the explosives against the Crusades within [chapel courtyard](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Cathedral_Quarter) within the city.[30] Following this, Tess was amongst those that faced off against [Inquisitor Fairbell](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Inquisitor_Fairbell) within the [Light's Dawn Cathedral](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Light%27s_Dawn_Cathedral). With Fairbell's death, the Forsaken departed from the area, the Alliance successfully claimed Gilneas.[31]

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Tess_and_Genn_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg/320px-Tess_and_Genn_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg?b9ca19)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Tess_and_Genn_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg)

In the aftermath, Tess revealed that she noticed how Genn always hid from his pain and that she was always there for him, even though he never noticed. After acknowledging this and apologizing, Genn admitted that he found it hard to change as the world did, and that Tess had proven herself a thousand times over. Thus, he made the decision to abdicate his throne in favor of making his daughter queen.[7] While taking the time to process that her father had handed the kingdom to her, she directed Alliance champions to ensure that he was all right as he visited [Liam](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Liam_Greymane)'s grave.[32]

### The War Within

[![WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [novels](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels), [novellas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novellas), or [short stories](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Short_stories).**

After the destruction of [Dalaran](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dalaran) in 42 ADP, Tess was amongst the Alliance leaders who answered [Jaina](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jaina) and [Thrall](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thrall)'s joint meeting in [Boralus](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Boralus), where they helped organize the strike force that they could take to [Khaz Algar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Khaz_Algar). When [Lord Danath Trollbane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Danath_Trollbane) proposed to call on the [7th Legion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/7th_Legion) in the [Arathi Highlands](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Arathi_Highlands), she exchanged a glance with [Magister Umbric](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Magister_Umbric), but ended up accepting the proposal after listening to the opinions of the other leaders.[33]

[![Comics title.png](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Comics_title.png/57px-Comics_title.png?427e0a)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Comics) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [manga](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Manga) or [comics](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Comics).**

Months later, Tess spent [Winter Veil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Winter_Veil) with her parents visiting her brother's grave.[34]

### Blood Ties

[![WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels) **This section concerns content related to the *Warcraft* [novels](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novels), [novellas](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Novellas), or [short stories](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Short_stories).**

Following the [war in Khaz Algar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/War_in_Khaz_Algar), Tess and her mother attended the wedding of [Turalyon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Turalyon) and [Alleria Windrunner](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alleria_Windrunner) in [Suramar City](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Suramar_City).[35]

## Age

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Tess_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg/320px-Tess_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg?6183c1)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Tess_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg)

- A range of her age can be discerned from several details in books and comments in game:
  - In *[Day of the Dragon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Day_of_the_Dragon)*, [Daval Prestor](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Daval_Prestor) ([Deathwing](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Deathwing)) remarks that [Calia Menethil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Calia_Menethil) and [Jaina Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jaina_Proudmoore) were the only daughters of reigning monarchs who were both single and beyond infancy.[36]
  - Calia states that Tess was "very young" when Gilneas left the Alliance.[8] As Gilneas left the Alliance potentially before Anduin was born, there is only a range of about two to three years in which Calia could never have known of her.
  - She is noted as being near [Anduin Wrynn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn)'s age,[37] placing her at least in her mid twenties as of *[Dragonflight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight)*. It explicitly states she is 'a few years his senior'.
  - She appears in game during year 28, using the human female model rather than that of a child. At this point, Anduin was only thirteen, for her to be near Anduin's age, she would at most be a teenager. [Vanessa VanCleef](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Vanessa_VanCleef) also uses the human female model, despite only being a teenager by the time of *Cataclysm*.
- For her to be near Anduin's age, within several years, she could not have been alive during Daval Prestor's hunt for a bride, as that would be year 8. This would place her well out of range of Anduin, who would only be born nearly ten years later. As Anduin would come to use a modified version of an adult model around his sixteenth year, Tess using the human female model is not out of place.
  - With a generous estimate of 'near Anduin's age' and 'a few years his senior', likely around 2-3 years, she would have to have been born around year 13, as Anduin was born in year 15.
    - This would have made Tess anywhere from two to five years old when Gilneas left the Alliance; giving little reason for Calia to need to meet a toddler.
  - Using this estimate, she would have been around 15-16 in *Cataclysm*.
  - With this estimate, she would have been 19-20 in *Legion*.
  - She would be roughly 27-28 years old when she was made Queen of Gilneas; only two to three years older than Anduin.
